# L'Altro Spazio — Institutional & Public Information Architecture v1

**Working date:** 16 August 2026
**Status:** Decision document for the next phase of digital/institutional work. Defines what the public system must communicate and how that knowledge should be structured. Does not implement, redesign, or code the website.
**Builds on:** `docs/institution/laltrospazio-working-paper-v0.3.md` (canonical synthesis) and its supporting research. Repository documentation is canonical over chat summaries; this document does not restate Working Paper v0.3's evidence — it reads that evidence into architecture.
**Scope discipline:** No visual design, no component code, no route changes, no new public claims. Every recommendation below is checked against what the working paper actually establishes, not what would be convenient to publish.

---

## 1. Executive decision

L'Altro Spazio's public system should stop trying to answer "what is this place?" with one sentence, and instead be built around a small number of distinct, honestly-labelled **information layers** that different visitors combine differently. The organisation is not reducible to a venue, a cooperative, or a mission statement, and Working Paper v0.3's own central finding — that practice and legal entity are two different tracks that do not always move together — is exactly the design constraint the public architecture has to hold.

The core decision this document makes: **separate PLACE (what you can visit now) from ORGANISATION (who runs it and how) from PRACTICE/HISTORY (why it exists and what it has done) from CURRENT ACTIVITY (what's on)**, and let the homepage lead with the first and last of these — because that is what most visitors actually need — while making the other two reachable in one click for the visitor who wants them, without forcing anyone through them.

This is not a menu-naming exercise. It is a decision about which facts the system is allowed to assert together in one place, and which facts must stay administratively separate so the site never accidentally implies things the evidence doesn't support (a continuous cooperative since 2010, a settled accessibility guarantee, a delivered project that was actually only proposed).

## 2. What L'Altro Spazio must be legible as

Distilling Working Paper v0.3 §15 into working, non-slogan language, at four levels of compression:

**A. One-line functional description**
A cultural and hospitality space in Bologna, run by and with disabled and Deaf people, that builds environments — not just events — around who is actually in them.

**B. 2–3 sentence outsider explanation**
L'Altro Spazio is a bar, cultural venue and social cooperative in Bologna where disabled and Deaf people work as hosts, cooks, artists and designers — not as guests being accommodated. It grew out of two people's different routes into the same realisation: that exclusion usually isn't caused by disability itself, but by spaces, services and assumptions built without disabled people in mind. Today that practice runs through a venue on Via Nazario Sauro, seasonal work at Parco 11 Settembre, and a cooperative that is turning a decade of informal practice into a more durable institution.

**C. Institutional definition** (for partners, funders, public administration)
Cooperativa L'Altro Spazio is the current legal and operating body of a practice that began in 2014–2015 and has run continuously since, under several legal vehicles, across multiple venues in Bologna. Its method is to design hospitality, cultural and public-space environments around Deaf and disabled participation as a structural condition, not an added accommodation — applied to a bar and event venue (Via Nazario Sauro), a public-park cultural programme (Parco 11 Settembre, under a 36-month collaboration pact with the Comune di Bologna since May 2025), and a growing set of services (training, accessible event design, Cena al Buio as a corporate format, translation/LIS work) that the cooperative is now formalising through public-procurement registration and grant applications.

**D. Internal full definition** (for the organisation's own strategic use — this is where Working Paper v0.3 §15's full synthesis belongs, unabridged)
> "L'Altro Spazio is a decade-long practice of building and rebuilding social, cultural and hospitality environments around the people actually in them, carried by two founders whose two starting experiences — gaze and visibility; translation and mediation — converged into a shared practice concerned with space, structure and participation; expressed across at least five physical environments and one further concept that did not fully launch; operated through at least four distinct L'Altro Spazio legal entities, plus Associazione Farm's separate and overlapping history, none of which should be collapsed into one continuous company; and now, since roughly 2022, in the process of turning that accumulated practice into a durable social-cooperative institution with formal public-administration relationships, employment structures and service ambitions." (Working Paper v0.3 §15, verbatim)

**Test:** (B) is what most homepage visitors need. (C) is what a funder or Comune reader needs and can find one click away. (D) belongs in an internal/strategy context, or at most linked from a "read more / research" page for the reader who wants the whole argument — never compressed onto the homepage.

Deliberately avoided unless a specific practice justifies the specific word: "we believe in inclusion," "safe space for everyone," "empowering," "breaking barriers," "diversity and inclusion" as a slogan. Where the evidence supports a specific claim (Deaf staff as hosts, a lowered counter, a decade of Cena al Buio), say the specific thing instead.

## 3. Institutional layers

Derived from the evidence, not from a template menu. Each layer answers a distinct question, serves a distinct audience, and carries a distinct evidence standard.

### 3.1 PLACE
- **Question:** Where can I go, and what will I find there?
- **Who needs it:** Tonight-visitor, first-time visitor, anyone physically travelling there.
- **Belongs here:** Current address(es), how to get there, what it looks like/feels like, current opening status, menu, how to book, current accessibility conditions (see §9).
- **Does not belong here:** Legal-entity explanation, history, grant applications, programme theory.
- **Temporal character:** Current only. Never historical-as-current.
- **Evidence standard:** Owner-confirmed / current operational record only. This is the layer with the least tolerance for stale or inferred facts, because it directly drives a physical visit.
- **Form:** A page (or homepage section that is really this page compressed).

### 3.2 ORGANISATION
- **Question:** Who runs this, and what kind of body is it?
- **Who needs it:** Funders, Comune, partners, journalists, workers/collaborators, and any visitor who explicitly asks.
- **Belongs here:** Cooperativa L'Altro Spazio as the current operating entity, what a cooperative is in this context, governance in broad terms, the honest one-paragraph version of "this has operated under several legal forms since 2014" (not the five-entity detail — that's HISTORY).
- **Does not belong here:** Board composition detail that is currently unresolved (Working Paper v0.3 §16 — legal representative conflict after March 2026 is explicitly unresolved and must not be guessed at publicly), financial figures, historical entity-by-entity detail.
- **Temporal character:** Current, with a light pointer back to History.
- **Evidence standard:** Owner-confirmed current governance facts only; anything unresolved in the working paper stays unpublished.
- **Form:** A short page or a section of an "About" page — not a footer afterthought, because public-administration and funder readers specifically need this distinguished from PLACE.

### 3.3 APPROACH / METHOD
- **Question:** How does L'Altro Spazio actually work, and why does it work that way?
- **Who needs it:** Cultural partners, schools/researchers, journalists, companies considering a service, anyone who suspects this is "an inclusive bar" and wants to know if that's really all it is.
- **Belongs here:** The practice-not-venue framing (labelled as interpretation, per Working Paper v0.3 §10), the two founders' distinct routes into the work (gaze/visibility; translation/mediation — also labelled interpretation), concrete method examples (a beer tap adapted until a colleague could use it herself; a bilingual incorporation deed; staff who are Deaf or disabled as professionals, not curiosities).
- **Does not belong here:** A slogan version of the same ideas. Every claim here should be a specific, checkable instance, not an abstraction alone.
- **Temporal character:** Durable — this changes slowly, if at all.
- **Evidence standard:** Working paper synthesis, clearly labelled where it is interpretation rather than settled fact.
- **Form:** A page. This is the layer most at risk of becoming generic NGO "our values" copy; see §19's risk test.

### 3.4 HISTORY
- **Question:** How did this come to exist, and what has it been before now?
- **Who needs it:** People who knew Via Polese or Pratello, researchers, journalists, the organisation's own institutional memory, anyone asking "is that still open?"
- **Belongs here:** Via Polese (2014), the 2015 SRL and Via Nazario, Pratello SRLS and its two venues, JASPACE, da Osvaldo's closure, the cooperative's own formation and consolidation — told as a real timeline with real discontinuities, not smoothed into "since 2015 we have..."
- **Does not belong here:** Presenting any closed venue as if it were still reachable. Presenting the cooperative as continuous with 2010 or 2014.
- **Temporal character:** Historical. Explicitly and visually distinct from current-state pages.
- **Evidence standard:** Working paper's own DOCUMENTED/OWNER-CONFIRMED/CORROBORATED distinctions, preserved rather than flattened for public consumption. Discontinuities (Via Polese's forced closure, da Osvaldo's failure, JASPACE's uncertain fate) are told, not hidden (Working Paper v0.3 §11, §17).
- **Form:** A page, or a small dataset (a structured timeline) rendered as a page.

### 3.5 WORK (Programmes, Projects, Services)
- **Question:** What does L'Altro Spazio actually do, beyond hosting people for a drink?
- **Who needs it:** Cultural organisations, companies, schools, anyone assessing partnership or booking a service.
- **Belongs here:** Recurring programmes (Cena al Buio, Spazi Migranti, Aperitivo dal Mondo, the LIS-awareness course lineage), the Parco collaboration, services being formalised (training, accessible event design, corporate Cena al Buio), status-labelled current/past/proposed work.
- **Does not belong here:** Treating a submitted grant application as delivered work (Working Paper v0.3 §13 is explicit that SEGNI DI PACE, Sencity, and the 2026 Cineporto/Parco draft are proposals, not outcomes).
- **Temporal character:** Mixed — some entries durable (a decade-old format), some dynamic (this season's booking).
- **Evidence standard:** Status-labelled per §12 below; every entry carries a status tag, not just a description.
- **Form:** A dataset (structured records) rendered through one or two pages, not a single flat list.

### 3.6 EVIDENCE / LEARNING
- **Question:** What has this organisation learned, and how do we know any of this is true?
- **Who needs it:** Researchers, journalists, funders doing diligence, CripMinds-adjacent readers, the unusually curious visitor.
- **Belongs here:** A public-safe distillation of the working-paper method (evidence has authority levels; claims are labelled by how well-supported they are), selected case studies (Cena al Buio's ten-year lifecycle; the bilingual incorporation deed), an honest account of things that didn't work.
- **Does not belong here:** The full Working Paper v0.3 itself, confidential financial/legal detail, unresolved internal questions presented as settled.
- **Temporal character:** Durable, updated occasionally as research continues.
- **Evidence standard:** Only material the working paper marks safe for eventual public use; nothing marked DO-NOT-PUBLISH, UNRESOLVED, or confidential crosses this boundary.
- **Form:** Background layer at v1 (linked, not navigated to by default); see §11 for the internal/public boundary in detail.

### 3.7 CURRENT ACTIVITY (Events / Notices)
- **Question:** What's actually happening, and is the place open?
- **Who needs it:** Tonight-visitor, regular visitor, anyone checking before travelling.
- **Belongs here:** The existing `/eventi` registry and operational-notice system already built in this repo.
- **Does not belong here:** Programme explanation (that's WORK); historical event material.
- **Temporal character:** Fully dynamic.
- **Evidence standard:** Canonical event/notice records only, exactly as the existing pipeline already enforces.
- **Form:** Dynamic dataset, already implemented.

### 3.8 SERVICES / COLLABORATION
- **Question:** How can an organisation, company or public body work with L'Altro Spazio?
- **Who needs it:** Companies (training, Cena al Buio B2B), Comune/public administration, cultural festivals, schools.
- **Belongs here:** A concrete "work with us" surface listing status-labelled current services and an honest "we're building capacity in X, ask us" entry for proposed/experimental ones.
- **Does not belong here:** Treating MePA registration (a procurement-eligibility filing) as proof of active delivery volume (Working Paper v0.3 §13).
- **Temporal character:** Semi-durable.
- **Evidence standard:** Current-service claims require current evidence; everything else stays labelled proposed/experimental.
- **Form:** A page, drawing on the WORK dataset filtered to current/available items.

**What this set deliberately excludes:** a generic "Mission/Vision/Values" layer (folded into APPROACH, told through specific practice rather than abstraction) and a generic "Impact" layer (no verified impact statistics exist per Working Paper v0.3 §16 — this is a verification-queue item, §14 below, not a page to build now).

## 4. Audience model

Not every audience needs equal homepage weight. Ranked by what the homepage must serve directly versus what should be one click away.

| Audience | Primary need | Homepage weight | Primary landing layer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tonight/soon visitor | Is it open, where, what's on | **High** — this is the homepage's main job | PLACE + CURRENT ACTIVITY |
| Someone who heard "inclusive bar" and wants more | Is that all it is? | **High** — second homepage job | APPROACH, one click from PLACE |
| Deaf visitor | Communication/access specifics | Medium — must be reachable in one click, not buried | ACCESSIBILITY (part of PLACE) |
| Disabled visitor (other access need) | Practical physical/sensory conditions before arriving | Medium — same as above | ACCESSIBILITY |
| Cultural organisation (partnership) | What could we do together | Low-medium — a clear path exists, not homepage-prominent | WORK / SERVICES |
| Comune / public administration / funder | What kind of organisation is this | Low-medium — needs ORGANISATION reachable, not assumed unnecessary | ORGANISATION |
| School / university / researcher | Depth, evidence, history | Low on homepage, but a real path must exist | EVIDENCE / HISTORY |
| Company (training, Cena al Buio B2B) | Is there a bookable offer | Low-medium | SERVICES |
| Journalist | Verifiable facts, contact | Low on homepage, high findability | ORGANISATION + contact |
| Potential worker/collaborator | Is this a place I could work | Low | ORGANISATION / "work with us" |
| Person interested in history | Full narrative | Low on homepage, deep once found | HISTORY |
| CripMinds-adjacent reader | Context, not a funnel | Minimal — footer/related-research only | EVIDENCE (contextual link only, see §11, §13) |

The homepage is not required to represent every audience equally; it is required to make every audience's actual next step reachable within one or two clicks, without making the tonight-visitor wade through institutional material to get there.

## 5. Place vs organisation

The everyday ambiguity — "L'Altro Spazio" meaning a place, a public identity, a historical practice, and a legal cooperative all at once — is a real information problem the working paper surfaces explicitly (§17: "preserve entity distinctions in public communication... without needing a reader to parse five entities' worth of nuance").

**Resolution: layer the answer, don't force a choice.**

- The visitor-facing surface (PLACE, homepage, event pages, menu) uses "L'Altro Spazio" exactly as a visitor would — a place you go to, full stop. No entity name, no legal-form explanation, ever required to understand "I'm going to L'Altro Spazio tonight."
- Exactly one sentence, present but not prominent (footer, or the bottom of an About/Organisation page), does the disambiguation for the reader who needs it: *"L'Altro Spazio is the public name of a practice that has run under several organisations since 2014; it is currently operated by Cooperativa L'Altro Spazio."* This single sentence is the entire public burden the average visitor carries.
- The ORGANISATION page carries the fuller, still-compressed institutional definition (§2C above) for the reader who explicitly wants it.
- The HISTORY page carries the full entity-by-entity account (Via Polese/Associazione L'Altro Spazio → SRL → Pratello SRLS → Cooperativa), because that is exactly where a reader asking "wait, which company is this?" ends up, by their own choice, not by being routed there.

This means the site never has to choose between "friendly place" and "legally precise institution" language in the same sentence — it assigns each register to a different layer and links between them.

## 6. Current vs historical model

A visitor-facing rule set, derived directly from Working Paper v0.3 §10, §11, §15:

**Current (may appear on PLACE, ORGANISATION, WORK, CURRENT ACTIVITY):**
- Via Nazario Sauro 24/F, Bologna
- Parco 11 Settembre (seasonal; explicitly labelled seasonal, never presented as identical in kind to the year-round Via Nazario venue)
- Cooperativa L'Altro Spazio (as of registration 27 June 2022; operating both Via Nazario and Parco since the 2025 board actions)
- Currently-evidenced programmes only (Cena al Buio, Aperitivo dal Mondo, Spazi Migranti, the LIS/Deaf-programming thread) — each still needs a current-evidence check before a specific claim like "runs every month" is published (§14)
- Published events/notices from the existing pipeline

**Historical only (must appear on HISTORY, never presented as current or reachable "as if open"):**
- Via Polese 7 (2014, Associazione L'Altro Spazio) — closed
- L'Altro Spazio S.r.l. (2015 incorporation) — the entity that opened Via Nazario; the venue continues, the SRL's operating role was superseded by the cooperative via the 2025 affitto d'azienda
- L'Altro Spazio Pratello SRLS and both its venues (Pratello 29/A, 2018–Sept 2025; da Osvaldo, 2022–2024) — closed
- JASPACE — a 2024 proposed concept, status uncertain, never operational; presented as exactly that, not as a venue
- Pre-2026 organisational language patterns (the 2016–17 "inspirational disability" framing, the unhedged "first inclusive bar in Italy" claim) — usable as historical evidence of how the organisation talked about itself, never re-published as a current self-description

**Hard rule enforced by this model:** no page may name a historical venue (Via Polese, Pratello 29/A, da Osvaldo) without also stating, in the same breath, that it is closed and since when. Silence on closure status is exactly the "hiding discontinued environments" failure mode Task 3 warns against.

## 7. Proposed public information architecture

### Primary navigation
1. **L'Altro Spazio** (home)
2. **Visit** (PLACE: address, hours-when-verified, menu, access)
3. **What's on** (CURRENT ACTIVITY: events/notices — existing `/eventi`)
4. **Our approach** (APPROACH/METHOD)
5. **Work with us** (SERVICES/COLLABORATION, folding in relevant WORK entries)

### Secondary / footer navigation
- **The organisation** (ORGANISATION: cooperative, place/organisation disambiguation sentence)
- **History** (HISTORY: full timeline, entity account, discontinuities)
- **Research & evidence** (EVIDENCE/LEARNING: public-safe method and case studies — can be introduced later; not required for v1 launch, see owner decision 7 in §21)
- **Contact**
- Social links, legal/privacy as usual site footer content

### Page purposes
| Page | Purpose | Primary content source |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Establish place + current status + one clear next action per major audience | Homepage argument, §8 |
| Visit | Everything needed to physically go there | venue-record.json + accessibility model (§9) |
| What's on | Current events/notices | existing event pipeline |
| Our approach | What makes this different from a generic accessible bar | Working paper §2–3, §14 (labelled interpretation) |
| Work with us | Concrete services/partnership paths, status-labelled | WORK dataset filtered to current/available |
| The organisation | Cooperative identity, governance in safe terms, place/org disambiguation | ORGANISATION facts only |
| History | Full timeline and entity account | Working paper §4–13, §15 |
| Research & evidence | Method + selected case studies (phase 2) | Working paper, redacted for public safety |
| Contact | How to reach a human for anything not covered above | venue-record.json |

### Page relationships
- Home links out to Visit, What's on, Our approach, Work with us as primary actions.
- Visit links to Accessibility detail (a section, not a separate top-level page, at v1 scale) and to What's on.
- Our approach links to History (for the reader who wants the full story) and to Research & evidence (phase 2).
- Work with us links to The organisation (for a funder/partner who wants to know who they'd be signing with) and pulls status-labelled entries from the same WORK dataset that Our approach references for examples.
- The organisation links to History for full entity detail, and is the only page that needs to state the cooperative's actual registration date.
- History is a leaf for most visitors but a hub for researchers/journalists — links to Research & evidence once that exists.

### What is not a page
- "Mission/Vision/Values" — folded into Our approach as specific practice, not abstraction.
- "Impact" — not enough verified current evidence exists (§14); do not build a page around numbers that aren't there.
- A single generic "Accessibility" top-level page promising blanket accessibility — accessibility is a section of Visit plus event-specific fields, per §9, not a standalone claim page.
- Separate pages per historical venue — History is one page/timeline, not five venue pages, at this scale.
- A CripMinds page or section on the main site (§13).

### What should be dynamic data
- Events, notices, opening-status (existing pipeline).
- WORK entries' status field (current/past/proposed/experimental — changes as reality changes, even if the description text is durable).
- Accessibility fields that are event-specific (see §9).

### What should be long-form institutional content
- Our approach, History, Research & evidence — durable prose, updated only when the underlying research or organisational reality changes, not on a content-calendar cadence.

## 8. Homepage information argument

Not a visual design — an ordered content logic, each block justified by what question it answers and where it sends the visitor.

**First 5 seconds — establish PLACE + current status.**
What: name, one-line functional description (§2A), current open/closed/notice status, address.
Why first: this is what the largest single audience (tonight-visitor) needs, and it's the fastest fact to verify/falsify, which builds trust immediately.
Sends the visitor to: Visit, or directly to the notice/hours if there's something to know right now.

**Next 30 seconds — establish that this is not just a bar.**
What: the 2–3 sentence outsider explanation (§2B) — specific practice (Deaf/disabled staff as hosts, not accommodated guests), not slogan language.
Why here, not first: a visitor deciding whether to go tonight doesn't need this to decide; a visitor who already knows the address but wants to understand what it is does. Putting it second, not first, respects both.
Sends the visitor to: Our approach (if they want more) or What's on (if they're now convinced and want to act).

**Next block — what's actually happening now.**
What: next event(s)/notice from the existing CURRENT ACTIVITY pipeline, with its already-correct empty-state behavior when nothing is confirmed.
Why here: converts interest into a concrete action (come to X), and honestly shows an empty state rather than inventing programming, consistent with existing `public-information-architecture.md` behavior.
Sends the visitor to: the event/notice detail, or `/eventi`.

**Next block — one clear path per non-visitor audience, not a full menu.**
What: two or three short prompts, e.g. "Looking to work with us?" → Work with us; "Curious how this started?" → History/Our approach. Not an exhaustive audience list — just enough that a funder, partner or researcher doesn't feel unaddressed.
Why here, not higher: these audiences are a minority of homepage traffic and can tolerate scrolling; forcing them above the fold would push out the tonight-visitor's information.
Sends the visitor to: Work with us, Our approach, The organisation.

**2 minutes — the visitor who's still here wants depth, not more marketing.**
What: nothing new is introduced at this depth on the homepage itself — by 2 minutes the homepage's job is done, and a genuinely curious visitor should already be on Our approach or History, reading real content rather than a longer homepage.

**Explicit non-goal:** the homepage is not a compressed version of every page. It establishes place/status, establishes specificity (not-just-a-bar), and routes. It does not attempt to summarize History, does not carry accessibility detail (that's Visit's job), and does not carry the working paper's propositions or method (that's Our approach's and Research & evidence's job).

## 9. Accessibility architecture

No blanket "accessible" claim. Modeled as separate dimensions, each with its own stability, verification and communication rules — treated as operational information, not institutional self-praise, per the brief.

| Dimension | Stable venue info (durable) | Event-specific info (dynamic) | Current verification needed before publishing | Uncertainty handling | Concrete-question path |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Physical access | Step-free route, door width, toilet accessibility | Whether a specific event's layout changes this | Current physical survey at Via Nazario and (separately) Parco | State "to be confirmed" rather than omit or guess | Direct contact prompt: "ask us before you come" |
| Visual access | Braille menus (historically documented, 2018 Lonely Planet), lighting/contrast | Whether a specific exhibition/event has tactile or audio-described elements | Confirm Braille menu availability is current, not historical-only | Same | Same |
| Deaf / sign-language / communication access | Whether staff currently include LIS-fluent hosts | Whether a specific event has LIS interpretation, subtitling, or is Deaf-led | Confirm current staff LIS capacity; do not assume 2018-level historical claims still hold | Same | Same |
| Hearing / audio access | Ambient noise level, whether visual alerts exist | Whether a specific event uses amplified sound in ways relevant to hearing-sensitive visitors | Not currently evidenced; treat as open | Same | Same |
| Cognitive / information access | Whether menus/signage use plain language, predictable layout | Whether a specific event is described in advance in enough detail to plan for | Not currently evidenced; treat as open | Same | Same |
| Digital access | Whether the website itself meets basic accessibility standards (this is a build requirement, not a claim — see AGENTS.md: "preserve accessibility as a design-system requirement, not a marketing layer") | N/A | Ongoing engineering responsibility, not a research question | N/A — this is enforced, not claimed | N/A |
| Event-specific access | N/A | Whether a specific event/programme (e.g. Cena al Buio) has its own access model beyond the venue baseline | Per-event, at event-authoring time | Event record should carry its own access fields, defaulting to "not specified" rather than inheriting an assumed baseline | Contact prompt on the event page |
| Assistance / contact | A single, real contact channel for access questions | N/A | Confirm which channel (phone/email/form) the organisation actually wants to use for this | N/A | This dimension *is* the concrete-question path for every other dimension |
| Temporary limitations | N/A | Whether today/this week something is out of service (a lift, a route) | Requires an operational-notice-style mechanism, reusing the existing notice model | Treat exactly like the existing operational-closure notice system | Same notice surface |

**Structural recommendation:** implement accessibility as a small structured schema (per-dimension fields on the venue record, plus optional per-dimension override fields on event records), not free-text marketing copy — this both prevents the "one badge says accessible" failure mode and gives the WORK/EVENT pipeline a natural place to attach event-specific access detail. This is a data-model implication for the next phase (§23), not something to build now.

## 10. Programme / event / project / service model

Explicit distinction, because the existing site already has event infrastructure that must not be conflated with programme identity:

- **PROGRAMME / FORMAT**: a durable, named practice with its own history and mechanism (Cena al Buio, Spazi Migranti, Aperitivo dal Mondo, the LIS-awareness course lineage, Cineporto BOff at Parco). Described on WORK. Has a status (current/past) and, where relevant, a short lifecycle note (e.g. Cena al Buio: a decade-old format, unchanged mechanism, one unexplained 2024 promotional gap — told honestly per Working Paper v0.3 §8, §11).
- **EVENT**: a specific, dated occurrence of a programme, or a one-off. Lives in the existing `content/events/` pipeline. A specific Cena al Buio night is an event; it links to the Cena al Buio programme entry for context, and carries its own date, booking info and (per §9) its own access fields.
- **PROJECT**: a bounded, often externally-funded undertaking with a defined scope and (usually) an end state — SEGNI DI PACE, Sencity, the Cultura Verde-funded Porto Verde work. Distinguished from a programme by having a funding/application lifecycle rather than being an ongoing house format. Carries a status per §12 (proposed/awarded/delivered/archived).
- **SERVICE**: something L'Altro Spazio offers to be booked or contracted by an external party — training, accessible event design consultancy, Cena al Buio as a corporate B2B offer, translation/LIS work. Distinguished from a programme by being offered *to* someone rather than run *for* the general public. Lives on Work with us / Services, drawn from the same underlying WORK dataset.
- **HISTORICAL FORMAT**: a programme or venue-specific practice that has ended (aperitivo al buio's Via Polese-era ancestor forms, the "Prove al Buio" interim name, Guasto Village's container-bar season). Lives on History, not WORK.

**Linking model:** one shared WORK/PROGRAMME dataset is the source of truth for programme identity (name, description, status, lifecycle note); EVENT records reference a programme by ID where applicable but are the canonical source for date/booking/access specifics; PROJECT and SERVICE records are separate types in the same dataset family, distinguished by their status vocabulary (§12) rather than being a different system. This lets a canonical event page say "this is a Cena al Buio night — here's what Cena al Buio is" without duplicating the programme description into every event record, and lets Work with us pull "services currently available" as a filtered view of the same data rather than a hand-maintained separate list.

## 11. Evidence and learning layer

**What should remain internal (v1 and likely permanently):** the full Working Paper v0.3 and its evidence identifiers (C-FB-, C-ORIG-, S-numbers), all confidential financial/legal/personnel material (already walled off by the working paper's own Appendix B), unresolved questions (current legal representative, current workforce composition, current accessibility audit), any material still labelled UNRESOLVED, CONFLICTING, or DO-NOT-PUBLISH in the working paper's own vocabulary.

**What could eventually be public**, once separately drafted and safety-checked against the working paper's status labels — not now, not as part of this document:
- A short "how we work" essay drawing on Our approach but going deeper into method (the beer-tap adaptation story, the bilingual incorporation deed, staff participating in the venue's own construction) — genuinely distinctive material that does not require inventing anything.
- A small number of case studies told as case studies (Cena al Buio's ten-year lifecycle; the LIS-programming thread's decade of maturation) — these are exactly the "specific, checkable instance instead of a slogan" material Working Paper v0.3 §17 recommends building future communication from.
- An honest account of discontinuity (Via Polese, da Osvaldo, JASPACE) at the level of organisational lesson, matching exactly what §11 of the working paper already licenses to say publicly (the high-level fact and lesson, never the private detail behind it).
- Selected oral-history material from the interview corpus and origin narratives, used "carefully and with consent" as the working paper itself recommends (§17), and only with the speakers' explicit sign-off on public use.

**Function test for this layer:** it exists to let a researcher, journalist or unusually curious visitor verify that the organisation's self-description is earned, not to serve as a second homepage. At v1 scale, a single "Research & evidence" page (or even just a well-labelled link from Our approach) is enough — this does not need its own information architecture yet.

## 12. Status model for work/services

To prevent a proposal from ever reading as delivered work (the exact risk named in Task 9 and Task 13):

| Status | Meaning | Public display rule |
|---|---|---|
| CURRENT SERVICE | Actively offered and bookable now | May be presented as available, with a booking/contact path |
| CURRENT PROGRAMME | Actively running now, with recent evidence | May be presented as ongoing |
| RECENT WORK | Delivered within roughly the last cycle, not necessarily still running | Presented in past tense with a date |
| PAST WORK | Historical, concluded | Lives on History, not WORK |
| PROPOSED | Submitted or drafted, not yet awarded/agreed | Never presented as delivered; may be mentioned as "we're developing..." if the organisation wants to signal direction, always dated and hedged |
| EXPERIMENTAL | Piloted, uncertain continuation (JASPACE-class) | Presented exactly as uncertain, never smoothed into a settled offer |
| APPLICATION / PENDING | Grant/procurement filing submitted, outcome unknown | Not published as public content until resolved, per Working Paper v0.3's own discipline around SEGNI DI PACE, Sencity, MePA |
| ARCHIVED | No longer offered/relevant | Removed from WORK, may remain in History if institutionally significant |

This status field is mandatory on every WORK/PROGRAMME/PROJECT/SERVICE record — no record without one.

## 13. CripMinds relationship

Working distinction (already established, not repository-derived): CripMinds uses disability perspectives to make hidden systems legible; L'Altro Spazio materially creates and lives inside the alternative. These are related but distinct undertakings by the same people, not one brand extended into two audiences.

**Recommendation: minimal, contextual, non-primary.**
- No CripMinds content, funnel, or cross-navigation item on L'Altro Spazio's primary or secondary navigation.
- At most, a single footer-level "related research" link, if and only if the organisation wants one — framed as "a separate project examining disability and systems," not as "read more from us."
- No merged identity anywhere a visitor could mistake one project for a sub-brand of the other.
- If a CripMinds essay ever directly analyzes L'Altro Spazio itself (using it as a case study), that essay may link *to* L'Altro Spazio's site — the traffic direction should run CripMinds → L'Altro Spazio contextually, not the reverse, since L'Altro Spazio is the more concrete, visit-driven public asset and should not be diluted into a research funnel.

This keeps CripMinds' credibility (an independent analytical voice) and L'Altro Spazio's credibility (a concrete, lived practice) from contaminating each other.

## 15. Content authority

| Public information family | Authority |
|---|---|
| Venue address, coordinates, canonical name | Existing canonical venue record (`content/venue.json` / `docs/project/venue-record.json`), per existing field-authority model |
| Opening hours | Owner / operational schedule / Google Business Profile, once access is confirmed — per existing `field-authority.md` |
| Events | Canonical event registry (`content/events/`) — never social/directory content directly |
| Historical facts | Working Paper v0.3 and its cited evidence registers | 
| Current accessibility | Current venue verification (owner-confirmed, field-by-field) — never historical evidence alone, never Facebook/old posts |
| Programmes/services | Working Paper v0.3 for historical/lifecycle framing; owner confirmation for current status |
| Projects/grants | Formal award decision or signed agreement — a submitted application is not authority for "awarded" or "delivered" |
| Partnerships | Signed/confirmed relationship — a partner's adhesion letter for one project is not authority for an ongoing partnership |
| Organisation/governance | Current registry record or owner confirmation — not an inference from board minutes that conflict with each other (Working Paper v0.3 §16, C040–C041) |

**Standing rule carried forward from the working paper's own method:** Facebook or social-media history is never authority for a *current* fact, however useful it is as authority for a *historical* one. This distinction is the single most important inherited discipline for the public architecture.

## 17. Institutional-memory flow

Extends the provenance philosophy already in the repo (`content-provenance.md`, `field-authority.md`) to cover the new content families this architecture implies:

```
SOURCE (owner statement, formal record, current verification, Facebook archive only for historical claims)
  -> CANDIDATE / RECORD (a field or record with a status/authority tag, exactly as venue-record.json already models)
  -> REVIEW (owner confirmation, matching the existing candidate-review discipline used for events)
  -> CANONICAL INSTITUTIONAL FACT (promoted only after review, carrying its authority level and timestamp)
  -> PUBLIC CONTENT (rendered only from canonical facts whose status permits publication)
  -> EXPIRY / UPDATE (re-verification trigger — a season ends, a grant resolves, a venue closes)
```

Applies to: future events (already implemented), grant/project outcomes (SEGNI DI PACE resolving one way or another), programme status changes (Cena al Buio's cadence being reconfirmed), partner changes, accessibility changes, service launches, public-space agreement renewals (the Parco pact's own review points), and, only if the owner chooses to publish one, a team/staff record.

This is a conceptual model, not a CMS to build now — it says new knowledge should enter through the same source→candidate→review→canonical→public pipeline the event system already demonstrates, extended to a few more content families (WORK/PROGRAMME, ORGANISATION facts, ACCESSIBILITY fields) rather than inventing a parallel system for each.

## 19. Quality and risk test

Before finalising, the architecture above is checked against the ten failure modes it could plausibly fall into. A risk is marked RESOLVED only where a specific structural decision above (not just good intentions) addresses it.

| Risk | Present if... | How this architecture resolves it |
|---|---|---|
| **Generic NGO risk** — looks like Mission/Impact/Projects/Donate | A visitor could mistake the nav for any charity's template | RESOLVED: no Mission/Vision/Values page (§7 "what is not a page"), no Impact page (no verified statistics exist, §14), no Donate concept anywhere in this architecture (not requested by evidence or by the owner). Nav is built from the institutional layers in §3, not a template. |
| **Bar-reduction risk** — public model reads as primarily a bar | Homepage or nav leads entirely with hospitality | RESOLVED: the homepage's second block (§8, "next 30 seconds") exists specifically to establish this is not just a bar before the visitor leaves; Our approach (§3.3) is a primary-nav item, not a footer afterthought. |
| **Theory-overload risk** — a visitor needs the working paper to visit | Homepage or Visit page requires reading propositions/method first | RESOLVED: the homepage argument (§8) explicitly excludes working-paper propositions and method from the homepage; that depth lives only in Our approach and, optionally, Research & evidence (§11), never gating Visit or What's on. |
| **History-overload risk** — old organisational complexity swamps present-day use | A visitor trying to visit or book has to parse five legal entities first | RESOLVED: §6's current/historical model and §5's place/organisation layering mean a visitor never needs entity history to visit; the five-entity account lives only on History, reached by choice. |
| **Accessibility-washing risk** — easy to claim "accessible" without specifics | A single badge or blanket claim exists anywhere | RESOLVED: §9 models accessibility as nine separate dimensions with explicit "to be confirmed" states and no blanket claim page (§7, "what is not a page"); the schema recommendation structurally blocks a free-text "we are accessible" claim from being the only content. |
| **Entity-confusion risk** — visitor could think the cooperative existed since 2010 | ORGANISATION or PLACE content implies continuous legal identity | RESOLVED: §6's current/historical model states the cooperative's actual 27 June 2022 registration explicitly wherever the cooperative is named; §2's institutional definition (C) never claims continuity before the cooperative; History (§7) is the only place the pre-2022 entities appear, always dated. |
| **Proposal-as-result risk** — a grant application could appear as delivered work | A WORK/PROJECT entry with no status distinguishing submitted from delivered | RESOLVED: §12's mandatory status field (PROPOSED / APPLICATION-PENDING vs. CURRENT/RECENT/PAST) is required on every WORK record; §15's content-authority table states a submitted application is never authority for "awarded" or "delivered." |
| **Event/calendar-reduction risk** — current activity overwhelms institutional identity | What's on dominates the homepage or nav over identity/approach content | RESOLVED: §8's homepage order places identity (block 2) before the events block (block 3), and What's on is one of five primary-nav items, not the whole homepage. |
| **Institutional-bureaucracy risk** — formalisation removes the cultural/hospitality character | ORGANISATION-register language leaks into visitor-facing PLACE content | RESOLVED: §5's layering keeps visitor-facing language entirely in "place you go to" register; institutional/cooperative register is confined to The organisation and History, reached only by the reader who wants it. |
| **Evidence-blindness risk** — public claims could drift from source/provenance over time | No mechanism ties public content back to verified source over time | RESOLVED: §17's institutional-memory flow and §15's content-authority table require every public claim to trace to a canonical, authority-tagged source, extending the same provenance discipline already enforced for events; §14's verification queue prevents unverified fields from entering public content in the first place. |

No risk above required a structural revision beyond what §§2–18 already establish — each is resolved by a decision already made, not by a new mechanism invented at this stage. Where a risk's resolution depends on future discipline rather than a one-time structural choice (entity-confusion, evidence-blindness, proposal-as-result), that ongoing discipline is exactly what §17's institutional-memory flow and §12's mandatory status field are for.

## 21. Owner decisions required

Only genuine judgment calls the repository cannot answer on its own.

1. **DECISION:** Should the ORGANISATION page name a current legal representative at all, given the unresolved March 2026 conflict (Working Paper v0.3 §16)?
   **Why it matters:** Publishing a guess risks being wrong and looking careless; omitting entirely risks looking evasive to a funder/Comune reader.
   **Recommended default:** Omit a named individual; state only "Cooperativa L'Altro Spazio, registered [date]" until a current registry record resolves the conflict.
   **Alternatives:** Publish Nunzia Vannuccini (per the more recent internal budget record) with a footnote caveat; delay the ORGANISATION page entirely.
   **Consequence of deferring:** The organisation page launches without a named representative — a minor, low-risk gap, not a blocker.

2. **DECISION:** Should a public team/staff page exist at all, and if so, at what level of detail?
   **Why it matters:** No current workforce composition is verified (§14); a team page invites exactly the kind of unverifiable claim (staff composition, disability statistics) this document warns against.
   **Recommended default:** No team page at v1; individual staff may appear contextually (e.g. named in a programme's history) but not as a roster.
   **Alternatives:** A minimal "meet the founders" page (Nunzia and Jascha only, using already-public origin-narrative material with their consent); a fuller staff roster once current composition is verified and staff consent to public naming.
   **Consequence of deferring:** No loss — nothing in the current architecture depends on a team page existing.

3. **DECISION:** How much of the origin-narrative material (Nunzia/Jascha's personal stories) should ever become public-facing copy, and in what form?
   **Why it matters:** This is genuinely distinctive, non-generic material (Working Paper v0.3 §17 recommends using it), but it is currently prepared as internal research evidence, not cleared public copy, and involves personal biographical detail.
   **Recommended default:** Reserve for Our approach/History in compressed, owner-reviewed form; do not publish verbatim quotes from the origin documents without the founders' explicit sign-off on public use, sentence by sentence if needed.
   **Alternatives:** Full first-person publication as a "founders' story" page; withhold entirely and keep Our approach at the more abstract method level.
   **Consequence of deferring:** Our approach launches with concrete-but-impersonal method examples (the beer tap, the deed) rather than first-person narrative; upgradeable later without rework.

4. **DECISION:** Should Cena al Buio's current operating cadence be asserted at all before the 2024 promotional gap is explained?
   **Why it matters:** Publishing "Cena al Buio runs monthly" without knowing whether the 2024 gap was operational or just promotional risks a specific, checkable, and possibly false claim.
   **Recommended default:** Describe Cena al Buio as a decade-old, currently-offered format without asserting a specific cadence, and confirm cadence with the owner before adding one.
   **Alternatives:** Ask the owner now and resolve before any WORK content drafting begins; publish with a cadence and correct later if wrong.
   **Consequence of deferring:** WORK entry launches slightly less specific than it could be; low risk either way.

5. **DECISION:** Should the Parco/Cineporto relationship be described as a L'Altro Spazio programme, an Associazione Farm collaboration, or both, on the public site?
   **Why it matters:** The working paper is explicit that Farm's public branding (`#FARMAssociazioneCulturale`) persists years after any operational transfer, and that brand persistence and legal control are "two different, entirely compatible facts" (§8) — a public page has to pick a register without either erasing Farm's role or implying legal ambiguity the organisation doesn't want surfaced.
   **Recommended default:** Describe Parco as "a seasonal cultural programme L'Altro Spazio runs in collaboration with Associazione Farm and the Comune di Bologna," naming both without adjudicating legal-operator history.
   **Alternatives:** Credit only the cooperative (risks erasing a real, still-visible collaborator); reproduce the full Farm/SRL/cooperative transition history publicly (over-detailed for a visitor-facing page).
   **Consequence of deferring:** Parco content on Visit/What's on stays generic ("seasonal park activity") without a collaboration credit; low risk, easy to fix later.

6. **DECISION:** How should the 2022 "80% disabled staff" figure be handled if a visitor or journalist asks about it directly, given it must not appear as a current statistic?
   **Why it matters:** It is a real, dated, self-reported historical claim (Working Paper v0.3 §9, §12) that may resurface in press inquiries or old web content the organisation doesn't control (e.g. cached pages, old directory listings).
   **Recommended default:** Prepare a short, honest standing answer ("in 2022 we said X; we haven't re-measured since, and don't want to repeat an unverified number") rather than silence or repetition.
   **Alternatives:** Commission a current workforce-composition count and publish a verified current figure; decline to discuss the figure at all.
   **Consequence of deferring:** Risk of an inconsistent answer if asked before a standing line is agreed; low urgency, not launch-blocking.

7. **DECISION:** Should Research & evidence (the public evidence/learning layer) be built in this phase or deferred to a later phase?
   **Why it matters:** It is the layer most likely to either strengthen credibility (if done well, using the working paper's own case-study material) or produce theory-overload (if it leaks working-paper density onto a casual page).
   **Recommended default:** Defer to a clearly later phase; ship v1 without it, with Our approach absorbing the minimum necessary depth.
   **Alternatives:** Build a minimal one-page version now, using only the two or three strongest case studies (Cena al Buio's lifecycle, the incorporation deed); commit to never building it.
   **Consequence of deferring:** No loss at launch; the "Research & evidence" footer link in §7 simply doesn't exist yet, and nothing else in this architecture depends on it existing.

8. **DECISION:** What is the correct current framing for Via Nazario S.r.l.'s relationship to the cooperative today, on a public page (e.g. "operated by," "formerly operated by," or no mention at all)?
   **Why it matters:** The affitto d'azienda (March 2025) moved the operating business to the cooperative, but the SRL as a legal entity's current status/relationship isn't itself detailed in the working paper beyond that transaction.
   **Recommended default:** Public copy says "Via Nazario Sauro is operated by Cooperativa L'Altro Spazio" (current fact) and reserves the SRL's own history for the History page; no public page needs to explain the affitto d'azienda mechanism itself.
   **Alternatives:** Explain the lease mechanism publicly for full transparency (over-detailed for a visitor-facing register); omit the cooperative's operating role entirely (under-informative for a funder/Comune reader).
   **Consequence of deferring:** No practical loss; this default can be applied without further owner input, but is listed because it's a real choice being made on the owner's behalf.

9. **DECISION:** Should CripMinds appear anywhere on the public site at all, even as a single footer link (per §13's "at most" option)?
   **Why it matters:** This is a brand-boundary decision only the owner can make — the repository has no evidence either way about intended positioning.
   **Recommended default:** No CripMinds link at v1; revisit only if CripMinds publishes something that directly and usefully contextualizes L'Altro Spazio.
   **Alternatives:** A single "related research" footer link now.
   **Consequence of deferring:** No loss; easy to add later, harder to walk back if added and then reconsidered.

10. **DECISION:** Is the owner ready to commission a current, systematic accessibility audit (physical/sensory/communication/digital/programme) as a concrete near-term action, given how much of this architecture's Visit/Accessibility content depends on it?
    **Why it matters:** This is the single largest content gap the architecture surfaces (Working Paper v0.3 §16, §17 both flag it) and the one most likely to determine how soon Visit/Accessibility content can move past "to be confirmed."
    **Recommended default:** Commission a focused current-state audit (not a full working-paper-style research pass) covering just the fields in §9's table, scoped to Via Nazario first, Parco second.
    **Alternatives:** Publish Visit/Accessibility with most fields marked "to be confirmed" indefinitely; defer any accessibility content until the audit is complete.
    **Consequence of deferring:** Visit/Accessibility content launches thinner than the architecture allows for; not a blocker, but the single biggest lever for closing the gap between this architecture and a fully populated site.
