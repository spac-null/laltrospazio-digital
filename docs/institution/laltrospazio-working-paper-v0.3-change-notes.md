# Working Paper v0.3 — Change Notes

**Working date:** 15 August 2026
**Purpose:** Explain the major conceptual and evidentiary differences between Working Paper v0.2 and v0.3. This is not a line-by-line diff — see the working paper itself (`laltrospazio-working-paper-v0.3.md`) for full detail and evidence citations.

## Where v0.2 actually lives

Working Paper v0.2 and its evidence register were never committed to this repository — they exist only in the owner's local working files (`laltrospazio_working_paper_v0.2.md` and `laltrospazio_evidence_register_v0.2.md`). This session located both, read them in full, and used them as the required predecessor and companion research. **v0.3 is the first version of the working paper committed to `docs/institution/`.** v0.2 is unchanged and still available at its existing location; nothing in v0.3 alters it. If the owner wants v0.2 itself preserved inside the repository going forward, that would be a separate, explicit action — this session did not copy it in, since that was not requested.

## What v0.3 adds that v0.2 did not have

v0.2 was written before the Facebook archive (2014–2025, 930 posts) had been systematically analysed, and before three owner-origin narrative documents had been ingested. Both bodies of evidence were completed in the sessions immediately preceding this one and are frozen for this version. v0.3's entire contribution is synthesising that now-frozen evidence into a single narrative, alongside v0.2's existing documentary/institutional layer.

### 1. A real predecessor venue: Via Polese (2014)

v0.2 had no knowledge of Via Polese at all. v0.3 establishes it as a substantive founding event, not a footnote: a venue already called "L'Altro Spazio," run by a distinct association (Associazione L'Altro Spazio), where Jascha and Nunzia met in person for the first time, which closed abruptly after serious water damage — and whose closure is the direct, owner-confirmed cause of the 2015 SRL and Via Nazario. This changes the shape of the origin story from "founded in 2015" to "rebuilt in 2015 after a first incarnation closed abruptly." Note: this is a closure, not an established business failure — nothing in the evidence shows the underlying concept or venue was economically unsuccessful before the water damage forced it shut, and v0.3 no longer uses "failure" language for Via Polese (an owner-review correction applied 15 August 2026; see below).

### 2. Two more venues and a second legal entity: the Pratello SRLS

v0.2 knew only L'Altro Spazio S.r.l. (Via Nazario) and Cooperativa L'Altro Spazio. v0.3 adds **L'Altro Spazio Pratello SRLS**, incorporated 25 May 2018 — a separate 50/50 entity that ran two further venues: L'Altro Spazio al Pratello (29/A, Dec 2018–Sept 2025) and L'Altro Spazio da Osvaldo (96/A-B, 2022–2024, discontinued after proving economically unsustainable). Both are new to the institutional lineage and are kept explicitly distinct from L'Altro Spazio S.r.l. The documented lineage now runs to **four distinct L'Altro Spazio legal entities/vehicles** (Associazione L'Altro Spazio, L'Altro Spazio S.r.l., L'Altro Spazio Pratello SRLS, Cooperativa L'Altro Spazio), plus Associazione Farm as a separate, earlier and overlapping Parco/collaboration entity — Farm is not counted as an L'Altro Spazio vehicle.

### 3. JASPACE downgraded

An early reading of the Facebook material could have suggested a fourth venue. v0.3 is explicit that JASPACE was a January 2024 workspace concept with genuine preparatory steps (an interest form, an intended opening) but no established sustained operation — status: proposed/experimental, implementation uncertain, not a venue.

### 4. The 2015 incorporation dated precisely, and its deed read directly

v0.2 had only "2015" and owner memory for the SRL's founding. v0.3 dates it to 24 April 2015 from the deed itself, and reports — directly from the deed's own text, not from report — that it is a bilingual document (operative English text, Italian translation appended) recording Jascha's deafness and inability to read Italian. This is treated as strong formal evidence for Proposition 2, distinct from the founders' separately-preserved personal memory of the signing ("like marrying for love").

### 5. A decade-long concept/language-evolution argument

New to v0.3: a traced account of how the organisation's own public voice talked about disability and accessibility from 2014 to 2025 — including material (2016–17 "inspirational disability story" framing) that is uncomfortable by current standards and is deliberately preserved rather than smoothed over, alongside a 2024–25 pattern of increasingly generic "#Inclusione #Accessibilità" hashtagging. The working interpretation — that practice sometimes outran its own vocabulary, and vocabulary later sometimes outran practice — is new to this version and clearly labelled as interpretation.

### 6. 2024 accounting inactivity separated from 2024 activity

v0.2 described the cooperative as "essentially inactive" in 2024. v0.3 sharpens this using a Facebook post independently found in this pass: the cooperative's 2024 accounts do show no operating revenue, but a July 2024 post shows the cooperative-credited Cultura Verde project being actively delivered that same year, with the associated public funding liquidated in 2025. Accounting status and project activity are now treated as two separate facts.

### 7. The seven propositions evaluated individually, not just restated

v0.2 presented the seven propositions with supporting testimony. v0.3 evaluates each one against the fuller evidence base — noting where evidence is strongest (Propositions 2 and 3), where it is more experiential than corroborated (1, 5, 7), and where it remains partly hypothetical at institutional scale (4, 6) — and names a specific tension or limit for each rather than treating all seven as equally proven.

### 8. Two owner-approved interpretations formally introduced and bounded

The "practice of environments, not a single venue" reading and the "gaze/visibility + translation/mediation converging" reading are both used throughout v0.3, explicitly labelled as interpretations at every point they appear, and explicitly bounded against overreach (neither is a claim about continuous legal identity; the second is not presented as a rigid causal model).

## Owner-review correction pass (15 August 2026)

A first owner-review pass found several sentences that exceeded what the evidence actually establishes. Corrections applied, without changing the paper's structure, title or overall conclusions:

- **Da Osvaldo:** removed language implying the organisation was "not destabilised" by the failed expansion. It now states plainly that the failed expansion placed substantial strain on L'Altro Spazio Pratello SRLS and contributed materially to that entity's later contraction, without disclosing the underlying confidential financial/legal detail.
- **Via Polese:** "failure" language removed throughout. Via Polese is now described as an abrupt, externally forced closure (serious water damage), with an explicit statement that nothing in the evidence shows the underlying concept or venue was economically unsuccessful before that closure.
- **Opening night:** the Facebook "Stiamo aprendo!" post (19:14) is no longer used to imply the exact door-opening minute is known, or that it contradicts the owner's "about an hour late" memory. Both are now presented as different facts about the same evening.
- **2015 deed:** language implying the bilingual deed was "made necessary by deafness" corrected — the translation procedure follows from Jascha not knowing Italian; his deafness is separately documented and contextually relevant, but the two are no longer conflated.
- **First build team:** softened from "three independent sources naming the same team" to an accurate description of one narrative source naming the group, plus two separate partial points of convergence (three names also appearing as interview subjects; one name also appearing in a 2017 Facebook credit).
- **Environments thesis:** removed the implication that "the same two people" were present across the entire 2010–2025 lineage; Parco predates Jascha's involvement, and Via Polese was opened by Nunzia before Jascha joined. The thesis itself is preserved, now scoped accurately to "from the 2015 rebuild onward."
- **Entity count:** standardised to four distinct L'Altro Spazio legal entities plus Associazione Farm (a separate, non-L'Altro Spazio entity), replacing inconsistent "three distinct" wording used in the first draft.
- **JASPACE:** "never progressed beyond pilot stage" softened to "no evidence reviewed for this paper establishes progression beyond the preparatory/pilot stage" everywhere this appeared.
- **Seven propositions:** Proposition 3 no longer merges the 2023 priority-booking/wheelchair-routing policy with the 2025 Comune pact as if one source; Proposition 5 is rebalanced toward interview evidence and Daniel's self-determination testimony, with construction participation kept as weaker, supporting evidence only; Proposition 6's heading is restored to its original person/technology wording, with the beer tap and the Sub_Bar Show as primary examples and the SpaceX/mural material reframed as an explicit interpretive extension; Proposition 7 no longer treats the unfinished opening night as primary evidence, leaning instead on repeated task-level adaptation and the Comune pact's monitoring mechanism.
- **Central synthesis (§15):** the "one through witnessing exclusion, one through living inside it" framing was too neat and has been replaced with the literal owner-approved formulation (gaze/visibility and translation/mediation converging).
- **§13 and §17:** removed normative commentary about what an organisation at this stage is "supposed to do," and corrected a sentence that misattributed an analytical conclusion about formalisation risk to the board records themselves.
- **Repository traceability:** created `docs/institution/research/v0.2-source-evidence-index.md`, a sanitised index covering only the specific S/O/C identifiers v0.3 actually cites (S01–S06, S13–S19, S21–S24, S26–S27, O01, C023, C040, C041). The paper's source note and Appendix A now accurately state that C-FB and C-ORIG evidence is fully repository-resolvable, while the v0.2-derived S/O/C identifiers are resolvable via this new sanitised index rather than the full off-repo v0.2 bundle.

## What is deliberately still open

All are carried forward as open questions, per the non-blocking protocol — not as blockers to publication or further work.
