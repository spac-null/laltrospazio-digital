# v0.2 Source & Claim Evidence Index (sanitised, repository-resolvable)

**Working date:** 15 August 2026
**Purpose:** Working Paper v0.3 cites S-numbers, O01 and C0-numbers that originate in Working Paper v0.2's own Appendix A (source list) and evidence register, both of which remain outside this repository, in the owner's local working files. This index makes the specific identifiers v0.3 actually relies on understandable and traceable **inside** the repository, without importing the underlying off-repo documents or any confidential material they may contain.

**This is an index, not a duplicate working paper.** It does not reproduce long passages, financial figures, or private content from any source. Where a source is a financial statement, board minute or filing, this index describes only what kind of document it is, roughly when it dates from, and what it supports at a high level — the same level of detail already used in Working Paper v0.2 and v0.3 themselves.

**Confidentiality:** none of the entries below include debts, creditors, litigation detail, bank data, tax detail, payroll, personal contact information, private correspondence, Messenger content, or private AI-tool excerpts. Several sources below (S13, S29-adjacent administrative material referenced in v0.2 but not used by v0.3) are financial/administrative in nature; only their safe, high-level evidentiary role is described.

## What this index does not do

- It does not reproduce the full v0.2 source appendix (S07–S12, S20, S25, S28–S30) or claim register (the remaining C001–C050 entries) — only the identifiers Working Paper v0.3 actually cites are included, per the instruction that this be an index of what is used, not a full duplicate.
- It does not import, quote, or summarise any confidential financial, legal, banking, tax, payroll, or personal-contact material from any off-repo source.
- It does not upgrade any source's evidentiary status. A source listed here as owner-held/off-repo remains exactly as authoritative, and exactly as limited, as Working Paper v0.2 already established — this index changes where the description of the source lives, not what the source can prove.
- It should be extended, not replaced, if a future version of the working paper relies on additional v0.2 source or claim identifiers.
