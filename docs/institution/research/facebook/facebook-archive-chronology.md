# Facebook Archive Chronology, 2014–2025

**Working date:** 14 August 2026
**Source:** `facebook-archive-inventory.md` (methodology); per-year evidence in session working notes (`extracted/chunk_notes/*.md`).
**Evidence classes:** FB-A (announcement + corroboration) · FB-B (repeated contemporary posts) · FB-C (single explicit post) · FB-D (pattern inference) · FB-E (hypothesis/lead). See `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` for the full claim ledger.

This is a reconstruction from the organisation's own public Facebook activity only. It is not a substitute for Working Paper v0.2's primary/formal-record evidence — it is a parallel, independent, contemporary-public layer that in some places corroborates, in some places extends, and in a few places complicates that record. Read alongside `institutional-timeline-v0.3-draft.md` for the reconciled view.

## 2014 — Via Polese: the real predecessor venue

**Owner-correction update (14 Aug 2026 pass):** Via Polese 7 is not a Facebook anomaly. It was a real predecessor L'Altro Spazio venue, and the archive's account of it now sits alongside owner-confirmed history and independent testimony from Maurice's historical interview.

**OWNER-CONFIRMED account:** the Via Polese 7 venue was already called "L'Altro Spazio" and was operated by **Associazione L'Altro Spazio** — a distinct entity from Associazione Farm. Nunzia Vannuccini had opened it shortly before Jascha Blume became involved. Jascha was invited there by Nunzia to present/show his documentary "I Sign, I Live" — **this is where Jascha and Nunzia first met in person.** The venue operated only a few months and closed following **serious water damage**; Associazione L'Altro Spazio ceased shortly after the venue's closure. That closure is what led Jascha to propose rebuilding the project together from scratch at a new location — the direct origin of the new, separate, 50/50 L'Altro Spazio SRL and, from it, Via Nazario Sauro (see 2015, below).

**CORROBORATED TESTIMONY:** Maurice's historical interview independently remembers Via Polese as the *"primissimo Altro Spazio"* (the very first L'Altro Spazio), coming after a period the interview associates with Nunzia's **"Senza Nome"** ("without a name") project. Per owner clarification, Senza Nome and Associazione Farm belong to an earlier Bologna project/collaboration context predating Via Nazario — background, not a claim requiring further reconstruction; its own dates and details remain a minor archival unknown.

**ARCHIVE-SUPPORTED (Facebook), consistent with the above:**
- From **15 February 2014** ("Aspettando L'Altro Spazio… Work in progress!" — the account's first post, same day as registration) through **27 April 2014**, the account posts near-daily as an operating hospitality venue at Via Polese 7. "Da oggi siamo operativi!" (2 March) marks its opening.
- 31 Mar: a **Braille sensitisation course** is announced for 1 April — the earliest disability-specific programming in the archive.
- 9 Apr: the account shares a Swiss public-broadcaster article, *"Un locale per sordo-ciechi a Bologna"* ("A venue for deaf-blind people in Bologna") — FB-A, independent press corroboration that the venue was explicitly framed, within its first two months, around **deafblind** accessibility (a broader/different frame than the Deaf/LIS emphasis more visible from 2015 onward).
- 1 April: two same-day closure posts ("lavori in corso," then "problemi tecnici") — visible operational fragility, consistent with the venue's short, difficult operating life and eventual closure.
- 7 June: a single Twitter-sourced post — *"stasera al @cineporto_BOff: I Sign, I life [DOC, 52'] di Jascha Blume [NL]"*. Per owner narrative, Nunzia saw the documentary at a Rome film festival before inviting Jascha to Bologna; this 7 June post is a separate, documented Bologna screening at Cineporto BOff (no "Roma"/"Rome" mention appears anywhere in the 2014 archive material). Status: OWNER-NARRATIVE + NON-BLOCKING EXTERNAL EVENT for the Rome screening itself — a minor archival unknown, not investigated further. See `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` C-FB-01/follow-up #8.
- A non-Facebook marketing-materials file, `29_Marzo_2014_Bar_Senza_Nome_&_Altro_Spazio.mp4`, was reviewed and is retained as minor archival background only: it shows a "senza nome" caption naming a Via Belvedere address at one point and an "l'Altro spazio"/Via Polese caption at another, on the same footage. **Owner clarification: Senza Nome and Associazione Farm belong to an earlier Bologna project/collaboration context, with collaborations between these projects/people predating Via Nazario** — this is background, not a new branch of institutional history, and no organisational or legal relationship between the names should be inferred from it. See `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` C-FB-29.
- **The account then falls silent for 16 months**, 27 April 2014 → 26 October 2015 — consistent with a short operating life ending in closure (water damage), rather than an unexplained gap. Working v0.3 should treat this window as "venue closes, Associazione L'Altro Spazio ceases, Jascha and Nunzia decide to rebuild," not as a mystery.

**Status:** OWNER-CONFIRMED (existence, operator, closure cause, first-meeting significance) + CORROBORATED TESTIMONY (Maurice) + ARCHIVE-SUPPORTED (dates, framing, public posture). The exact legal-registration dates of Associazione L'Altro Spazio are not required to use this chronology and remain a non-blocking open item (see `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` C-FB-01).

## 2015 — Via Nazario Sauro opens

**Owner-correction update:** the post-Polese transition is now understood as significant and deliberate, not incidental: Polese closes → Jascha and Nunzia decide to rebuild the concept together from scratch → a new, **separate, 50/50** L'Altro Spazio SRL is formed → the new Via Nazario venue is built and opens **29 October 2015**.

**ATTRIBUTED / OWNER MEMORY (not institutional/legal evidence):** the owner recalls signing the SRL's founding deed in personal, almost romantic terms — "like marrying for love" — including a bell being rung at the notary's office after signing. This is preserved here as a vivid oral-history fragment that may be useful for a future oral-history section of the institution's story. It is a memory of feeling, not a documentary fact, and should not be converted into formal chronology language or treated as evidence of the deed's actual date or terms.

**Update (15 Aug 2026 targeted-verification pass): the deed itself has been located and reviewed**, upgrading the deed's own facts from reported to DOCUMENTED. The notarial deed (before notary Rossella Ruffini, Bologna, **24 April 2015**) is formally bilingual — its operative legal text is in English, "known to the parties and to the notary," with an Italian translation appended by the notary — and its own text records Jascha Blume as unable to read Italian but able to read/write English, and completely without hearing. This directly confirms, from the deed itself rather than owner report, the incorporation date (24 April 2015, refining "2015"), the 50/50 Vannuccini/Blume structure, and the translation-accommodation mechanism; cross-checked against the Bologna Chamber of Commerce's own public registry extract (12 May 2015). The bell/marriage framing remains ATTRIBUTED/OWNER MEMORY — the deed neither contains nor could contain it. See `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` C-FB-26 and `../origins-source-note.md` §11.

- **26–29 October 2015**: a dense, real-time countdown — "nuova stagione," "-2! Due giorni alla festa d'inaugurazione," and on 28 Oct: *"A L'Altro Spazio - il nuovo locale ideato e costruito a misura delle persone con disabilità… Apriamo giovedì 29 alle ore 19:00 in via Nazario Sauro!"* — culminating in real-time opening-night posts on **29 October 2015** ("Stiamo aprendo! Alé!"). This is FB-A-grade, near-exact corroboration of the owner-confirmed 29 Oct 2015 opening date already in Working Paper v0.2 (attributed there to Michele's interview, S02).
- Nov–Dec 2015: a fast-forming recurring-format calendar — weekly BIO/km-zero aperitivo (L'Orto Circuito), "Giovelicious" Thursday DJ nights, Saturday DJ sets (dj Maurice), Sunday "colapranzo"/brunch, yoga courses, **"L'Altra Musica"** courses, **BeQuadro music lab** (inclusive/adaptive music education), wall-painting art installation (Giulia Caliò/Anna Oppio/"The Honey Boat"), a migrant-workshop evening with Associazione Farm/Arte Migrante/Antoniano, and — the clearest single new-programme finding of this year — **"aperitivo al buio"** (10 & 17 Dec): *"il personale sarà non vedente e vi aiuterà a muovervi nell'ambiente"* — sighted guests served/guided by visually-impaired staff. This is the direct ancestor of what becomes "Cena al Buio."
- **Associazione Farm is an active, co-credited collaborator at Via Nazario itself** in late 2015 (a film screening for the International Day Against Violence on Women, tagged "L'Altro Spazio Associazione Farm L'Altra scuola") — complicating any simple "Farm did Parco, SRL did Nazario" separation from the very start.
- Press attention is immediate and substantial: TG3 Emilia Romagna (twice), Radio2 ("Miracolo Italiano"), SuperAbile magazine ("Quando l'accessibilità è di casa"), Il Resto del Carlino/Bologna Welcome (family-friendly venue listing).
- **2 Dec, radio interview**: Nunzia Vannuccini, credited as "(L'Altro Spazio e Associazione Farm)," states on air: *"Le persone con disabilità non sono fatte di cristallo: sono, appunto, persone."* — an early, explicit articulation adjacent to Proposition 1/5.
- "Inclusione" is used as a self-description for the first time (27 Oct); "a misura delle persone con disabilità" is the first explicit design-for-disability language in the account's own voice.

## 2016 — first full year; Pincherle relocation; RAMP beer

- Mid-June to end-July: programming **physically relocates to Giardino Pincherle** for the summer, an explicitly multi-stakeholder public-garden project (Comune di Bologna Iperbole, Civico 32, Garbo Giovani Architetti, Centro Antartide, Bicigrill, Associazione Farm). The announcing post credits Farm with **prior** "riqualificazione di Via Belvedere con Luci nella città" and "del Parco 11 Settembre con il progetto Cine-Porto BOff" — i.e. Farm was already running Cineporto BOff at Parco 11 Settembre *before* this 2016 post, confirming continuity rather than origin at Parco. Pincherle programming included "Cinema dell'inclusione" — a Deaf-subtitled, blind-audio-described open-air film screening (14 Jul).
- **16 Sept: RAMP beer launch**, co-created with **Ken Fisher**, a Deaf American brewer (Portland's "Grateful Deaf" brewery), explicitly framed as an accessibility-awareness product. Jascha Blume is described for the first time in the archive as *"filmmaker olandese sordo, titolare de L'Altro Spazio"* — his role (owner) and Deaf identity stated together in an institutional post.
- The same September post gives a full weekly-format grid for the new season: La Fabbrica Etichetta Indipendente concerts, cena al buio + Spazi Migranti on Tuesdays, Thursday world-food aperitivo, Sunday vintage market, and (from November) a **wheelchair-repair "ciclofficina"** run with AUSL Bologna mental-health/disability services.
- **1 May**: an explicit labour-rights framing — *"il tema del Primo Maggio… è il lavoro per le persone disabili… l'inclusione inizia proprio dal diritto al lavoro"* — the strongest systemic (vs. inspirational-individual) framing found in this period.
- 18–21 May: a satellite bar at the **Exposanità** disability-services trade fair (BolognaFiere), while Via Nazario stays open — direct visibility within the disability-services sector.
- **Contradiction flagged:** a 16 Sept 2016 post calls this "L'Altro Spazio compie un anno di vita nella sua nuova sede" (turns *one* year old) — inconsistent with the Oct 2015 opening otherwise well-established; a Jan 2016 post appears to mis-date its own three-months-ago anniversary reference too. Treated as an internal dating slip in the account's own posts, not a source conflict.

## 2017 — "primo bar inclusivo d'Italia"; Parco still Farm's

- **28 Oct/2 Nov: "L'Altro Spazio compie 2 anni"** — a direct anniversary post (photos in the matching album dated 6 Nov 2017), anchoring the opening to Oct/Nov 2015, consistent with 2015's own record.
- **2 Nov**: *"L'Altro Spazio è il primo bar inclusivo d'Italia"* — an explicit, unhedged, unsourced national-first claim. Treat as a self-description (FB-C), not a verified fact.
- Jan: Jascha Blume curates "Gisteren Morgen" at the **SetUp Contemporary Art Fair**, bio'd as *"artista e filmmaker olandese non udente"* with prior Van Abbemuseum and Seattle Deaf Film Festival honours — the fullest institutional biography of Jascha as a Deaf artist found in this period.
- Jul–Oct: a multi-week **Chef Rubio** (national TV personality) "ricette accessibili" video collaboration, made accessible to Deaf viewers and (new this year) blind/low-vision viewers via an app.
- Nov: participation in **HANDImatica**, a national assistive-technology biennial.
- **8 Sept: Parco 11 Settembre 2017 programme post** — explicitly Farm/"Cine-Porto BOff"-branded, with L'Altro Spazio credited only as thematic collaborator on disability/inclusion content (Silent/Deaf Disco, "#LAltraCittà" workshops, an accessibility-proposals booklet). **This is the clearest single piece of evidence that, as of September 2017, the owner-dated "~2018" Farm→SRL Parco transition had not yet happened** — Parco is still presented as Farm's programme.
- Recurring slogan across 2016–17: *"Ciò che sembrava impossibile, oggi è diventato possibile"* ("what seemed impossible has become possible") — an informal institutional motto in this period.

## 2018 — a pop-up, a hiring policy, a second venue announced

- 16 May–Oct: **Guasto Village**, a university-district urban-regeneration pop-up — L'Altro Spazio runs a shipping-container bar there through the summer, hosting jazz (Paolo Fresu) and poetry.
- **17 Aug, in English**: an explicit hiring-policy statement — *"According to Italian law 69/99, every 15 employees must employ at least one person with a disability. We make our choice to hire people with a disability first, and not as a last resort… inclusion and accessibility are not intended as abstract concepts, but are realized every day."* The strongest direct organisational policy statement on employment found anywhere in the archive.
- **22 Nov**: **Lonely Planet Italia**'s Bologna pocket guide profiles the venue by name — *"I menù sono anche in Braille, il bancone è più basso del solito, la lingua dei segni è un modo per ordinare"* — independent third-party corroboration of concrete accessibility design (Braille menus, lowered counter, sign-language ordering), tagging Jascha Blume and Nunzia Vannuccini directly.
- **25 May 2018: L'Altro Spazio Pratello SRLS incorporated** — a **separate legal entity** from L'Altro Spazio S.r.l., owned 50/50 by Jascha Blume and Nunzia Vannuccini. (OWNER-CONFIRMED, supported by CONFIDENTIAL SUPPORT internal records not reproduced here — see `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` C-FB-07.)
- **Dec 2018: a second venue, "L'Altro Spazio al Pratello" (Via del Pratello 29/A), opens**, operated through the new Pratello SRLS, with "Prove al Buio" parties from 27 Dec — *"anche in un contesto più piccolo si può fare dell'accessibilità e dell'inclusione il proprio leitmotiv."* **Not mentioned anywhere in Working Paper v0.2.** *Correction note: the owner's initial recollection placed this opening around December 2017; stronger documentary evidence (the SRLS incorporation date plus independent internal records) refines this to December 2018. December 2017 is not preserved as competing/equal evidence — it is superseded by the stronger record.*
- No public post anywhere in 2018 distinguishes Associazione Farm from L'Altro Spazio S.r.l. — the owner-dated Parco transition remains entirely non-public.

## 2019 — Pratello opens fully; "Cena al Buio" is named

- **24 Jan–2 Feb: L'Altro Spazio al Pratello formally inaugurates**, timed to Art City/Arte Fiera, hosting Jascha Blume's installation "Hush – Dialoghi Possibili." Two venues (Nazario Sauro + Pratello) now run in parallel.
- Jul: **Parco 11 Settembre / Cineporto BOff summer season** confirmed as a distinctly branded season with its own open/close rhythm (Taiji Quan session 17 Jul; "ultima giornata" 26 Jul, describing "una stagione ricchissima di musica, incontri, eventi… mostre, spettacoli").
- 2–3 Sept: Via Nazario Sauro's autumn reopening — establishing a recurring seasonal close/reopen rhythm that recurs in most subsequent years.
- **20 Nov: "Cena al Buio" appears by that exact name for the first time** — *"Verrete accompagnati al vostro posto e serviti da personale non-vedente"* — consolidating a lineage that runs back to Dec 2015's "aperitivo al buio," via Dec 2018's "Prove al Buio."
- Disability-culture content this year skews toward blindness/visual-impairment and general "disabilità" framing (e.g. accessibility activist Iacopo Melio honoured by President Mattarella) rather than Deaf/LIS-specific content — a fluctuation in emphasis year to year, not a discontinuation of either thread.

## 2020 — pandemic disruption; Nunzia's founding narrative

- Feb–Nov: repeated COVID-driven closures and reopenings, a pivot toward coworking/kids-space/"smart-working" framing (Oct), and amplification of public accessible-COVID-information resources (25 Mar: Fondazione Innovazione Urbana's **LIS-video, high-readability, and audio** emergency communications).
- 22 May, 12 Dec: confirms **L'Altro Spazio al Pratello** continues operating in parallel through the pandemic.
- **15/17 Nov: Nunzia Vannuccini posts a first-person founding narrative** (self-authored, cross-posted from Instagram, tied to the Comune di Bologna's 2020 participatory-budget process for the park): *"A 29 anni ho sognato che quel parco… potesse rinascere… Nel 2010 ho incontrato le prime persone sorde della mia vita che da lontano segnavano… oggi è la prima lingua di mia figlia."* This is the single richest piece of founding-narrative testimony in the entire archive, corroborating the ~2010 origin and giving it a deeply personal register (LIS as her own daughter's first language) four to five years before the working paper's earliest documented formal Comune/Parco relationship.

## 2021 — the pandemic trough

Only **6 posts** all year, all May–June, all about the **Parco 11/CinePorto Boff 2021 edition** (7 May–31 Jul). One quotes **Redattore Sociale** (a respected Italian disability-affairs outlet): *"Capiamo di essere sulla buona strada quando i bimbi del parco vengono al chiosco e ci chiedono l'acqua in Lis"* — vivid, externally-sourced evidence of LIS as ambient, normalised practice among hearing children at the park. The sparse record shows continuity of format, not crisis; it cannot by itself establish whether *operational* activity (beyond the park) contracted — flagged UNRESOLVED, matching Working Paper v0.2's own "pandemic and subsequent restructuring… UNRESOLVED" note.

## 2022 — recovery, RisiBO, and a further Pratello expansion

- Named recurring formats **"Spazi Migranti"** and **"Spazio Concerto"** launch/formalise (Oct); **"Aperitivo dal Mondo"** (world-food nights with refugee/migrant associations) becomes a flagship format.
- **28 Nov: "RisiBO! Conquistiamo l'accessibilità"** — results of an accessibility-mapping project with **UILDM Bologna** (Unione Italiana Lotta alla Distrofia Muscolare), a national disability-rights NGO.
- **20–22 Oct: "L'Altro Spazio da Osvaldo" opens.** *Correction note: this was not simply a naming/branding partnership.* It was a **further operating location of the Pratello SRLS**, at **Via del Pratello 96/A-B**, based on a business-lease arrangement (affitto di ramo d'azienda) — OWNER-CONFIRMED, with CONFIDENTIAL SUPPORT from internal records not reproduced here (see `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` C-FB-08). The announcing Facebook post, signed "Nunzia & Jascha," explicitly recaps the lineage: *"…si aggiunge al primo l'Altro Spazio aperto nel 2015 in via Nazario Sauro 24/F e all'Altro Spazio al Pratello nato nel 2018…"* — and states *"clienti e lavoratrici e lavoratori con disabilità, che già oggi sono l'80% dello staff"* (an explicit, self-reported, dated 80% disabled-staff figure — not independently corroborated). **This further Pratello-area location is also not mentioned anywhere in Working Paper v0.2.** *Note on source discrepancy: one internal narrative describes this second Pratello location as opening in 2023, while stronger incorporation/lease-chronology evidence — consistent with this Facebook post's own Oct 2022 date — places the opening in 2022. Both are preserved internally as CONFLICTING; 2022 is used as the working chronology (see evidence register).*
- No public reference anywhere to the cooperative's 27 June 2022 legal registration — the account's self-presentation remains entirely venue/programme-centric through year-end.

## 2023 — three venues at once; the richest disability-policy convening in the archive

- Confirmed (correcting a working hypothesis from 2022): **both Pratello addresses (29/A and 96/A) operate concurrently**, not sequentially — a 25 April post invites bookings "nelle nostre due sedi al 96/a e al 29/a," meaning **three simultaneous venues** run for at least this year.
- **25 April (Festa della Liberazione)**: an explicit, operational (not just rhetorical) accessibility policy — priority booking for disabled and neurodivergent people, plus wheelchair-routing directions. "Neurodivergenti" appears in the archive's own voice for the first time.
- Mar–May: **"Barocco a Porte Aperte,"** an 11-date curated baroque/contemporary-classical concert series — the most formally "high culture" programming thread found in the archive.
- **12 Oct: Disability Pride Bologna** participation, with explicit activist-register language (*"non vogliamo un mondo a parte, ma essere parte del mondo"*) and hashtags #disabilityjustice #disabilityrights — a step beyond the "inclusione" hospitality-register used earlier.
- **28 Nov**: the Comune di Bologna's **"Diversity Team"** co-hosts a book presentation on disability access to justice — the first named Comune internal unit found co-programming at the venue, ~18 months before the working paper's earliest documented formal Parco/Comune agreement.
- **3 Dec (International Day of Persons with Disabilities): the richest formally-programmed disability-policy event in the whole archive**, inaugurating a "Spazio Artistico" at Pratello 96/A: a university LIS professor (Prof. Pietro Celo, Univ. of Bologna) presenting *Il Piccolo Principe* in LIS; a debate on teaching LIS in primary school; a debate on "Città 30: una città accessibile per tutti" with the **Ente Nazionale Sordi** (the national Deaf-people's institution); closing with Associazione Raggiungere Odv.
- Still zero mention anywhere of "cooperativa."

## 2024 — the only cooperative self-reference in the archive

- Pratello-venue visibility **drops sharply**: "Nazario Sauro" is named ~72 times across the year's posts vs. ~7 combined mentions of all Pratello addresses. **Da Osvaldo (96/A-B) is discontinued in 2024** — OWNER-CONFIRMED: the location proved economically unsustainable, with costs materially higher than expected and income insufficient to sustain it. (Public-safe framing only; no financial figures, creditors, or legal-process details are reproduced here — see `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` C-FB-08.) No closure was ever announced on the public Facebook account itself.
- **A coworking/workspace concept, "JASPACE," was prepared in January 2024** — an interest-form was created for potential users with an intended near-term opening, alongside a related creative-workshop/"Studio Blume" concept. *Correction note: this should not be described as an established operating venue.* Sustained operation, duration, a meaningful user base, and whether it moved beyond pilot/experiment stage are **not established**. Status: **PROPOSED / EXPERIMENTAL / IMPLEMENTATION UNCERTAIN** (see `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` C-FB-09).
- **"Cena al Buio" is not mentioned at all in 2024** — an apparent one-year promotional gap between its 2022-23 presence and its 2025 return.
- **15–26 July: "Il Porto Ritrovato"**, a two-week children's-workshop and live-music programme at Parco 11 Settembre, explicitly stated: *"Programma realizzato nell'ambito del progetto Il Porto Verde di Bologna da **Coop L'Altro Spazio**, Associazione Farm, Ben Fatto - l'Altra scuola, L'Oca Verde e Associazione Officina Educativa."* **This is the only place in the entire 930-post archive where the account credits "Coop L'Altro Spazio" by name.** It independently corroborates Working Paper v0.2's C009/S15 claim (the cooperative's May 2024 Comune/Quartiere "Cultura Verde: concerti, arte e lettura nel Porto Verde di Bologna" award) — moving that claim from "award documented" to "award documented **and** delivery independently corroborated by a contemporaneous public post naming the cooperative as deliverer." Associazione Farm again appears as an active 2024 collaborator, not a historical predecessor only. *Correction note (accounting vs. activity): the cooperative's 2024 statutory accounts record no operating revenue and describe the cooperative as inactive in accounting terms — this does NOT mean no organisational activity occurred. Owner-confirmed: Cultura Verde project activity took place in 2024 as this post shows; the related public contribution itself was financially received/liquidated in 2025, after submission/reporting of eligible expenditure up to the award budget. Accounting/revenue status and project/organisational activity are separate facts and should not be collapsed into one another.*
- New partners: **Free Fitness Bologna** (sport/inclusion framing), plus cross-venue West African arts-education showcases (Djembe percussion, dance) at other Bologna venues (B.u.c.o, Art Factory) whose end-of-course shows are hosted/promoted by L'Altro Spazio.
- No mention anywhere of the board decisions, employment change, or affitto d'azienda that (per primary records) occur in March 2025.

## 2025 — international art funding, and total silence during the transition

- Jan: only **two** addresses now advertised — Via Nazario Sauro 24F and Via del Pratello 29A, consistent with Da Osvaldo's 2024 discontinuation and JASPACE never progressing beyond its early-2024 pilot stage.
- **Pratello 29/A (operated through L'Altro Spazio Pratello SRLS) remained open until approximately the end of September 2025** — OWNER-CONFIRMED closure timing. This chronology does not include any insolvency, legal-process, or financial-distress detail behind that closure; only the timing itself is recorded here as institutionally relevant.
- **9 April: "The Sub_Bar Show #1"** — an internationally-touring (~30 cities) sensory-music event where "Artisti Sordi e udenti esplorano… attraverso l'uso innovativo dei subwoofer: il TATTO" (Deaf and hearing artists explore touch via subwoofers).
- **18 April: "Sensory Dialogues"** — a Deaf/hearing collaborative-performance residency, explicitly funded by the **European Union and the Goethe-Institut** via the **"Culture Moves Europe"** mobility programme — the most institutionally significant *international* cultural funding found anywhere in the archive.
- **A ~3.5-month total posting silence, 27 May → 11 Sept**, spans part of the same broad period in which, per primary (non-FB) records, the board approved Jascha Blume's employment and the affitto d'azienda (March 2025) and the Comune signed the 21 May 2025 Parco pact. **None of this is visible on the public account at all** — it resumes with generic post-summer reopening language, no reference to any transition. *Correction note: the owner does not know why this silence occurred. Status is UNKNOWN. Do not infer that it was caused by the SRL→cooperative transition, a crisis, a closure, operational inactivity, or a deliberate communications strategy — none of those causes is established, and the silence should not be read as evidence for or against any of them. The only safe methodological point is the general one: Facebook silence is not evidence of organisational inactivity, in either direction.*
- Sept onward: "#Accessibilità #CulturaAccessibile" hashtags attach even to routine reopening/DJ-night posts (a "blanket hashtag" pattern already visible in 2023-24); "Aperitivo dal Mondo" continues at high, near-weekly frequency (Tunisia, India, Spain, Sudan, Egypt, Mexico); **"Cena al Buio" returns strongly for Halloween** (27–31 Oct), unchanged in core concept a decade after its 2015 origin.
- **10 Nov, "Karaoke Ignorante con la Lu"** — ordinary party-flyer copy states: *"Sentire e ascoltare non sono la stessa cosa"* ("Hearing and listening are not the same thing") — the clearest instance anywhere in the archive of the seven propositions' underlying logic appearing spontaneously in commercial copy, not policy language.
- Archive ends 18 December 2025.

## Cross-year pattern: legal/governance invisibility

Across all twelve years, the public Facebook voice is **remarkably continuous through every documented legal and governance change** — the Farm→SRL Parco transition (~2018), the cooperative's registration (Jun 2022), the 2018 and 2022 Pratello-entity incorporations, and even the major March–May 2025 transition are essentially invisible in the account's own posts. The single exception in the entire archive is the July 2024 "Coop L'Altro Spazio" Porto Verde credit line. This is a consistent, cross-checked pattern (found independently in the 2018-19, 2020-22, 2023, 2024 and 2025 evidence) rather than an artefact of any one year's reading — see `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` for the claim-by-claim ledger. The 2025 posting silence (above) is the clearest single instance of this pattern, and its cause remains UNKNOWN — it should be read as an illustration of the general methodological point, not as evidence for any specific explanation.

## Interpretive note for Working Paper v0.3 review (status: INTERPRETATION, not fact)

Correction pass owner testimony (Via Polese → Nazario) plus the venue register in `programme-history.md` together suggest a research hypothesis that goes beyond any single claim above:

> **L'Altro Spazio may be better understood as a practice of creating and reconfiguring social/cultural/hospitality environments than as a single fixed venue.** Across the archive, the same name, the same people, and (arguably) the same underlying method recur across Via Polese (2014), Via Nazario Sauro (2015–), Parco 11 Settembre/Cineporto (pre-2014–), Pratello 29/A (2018–2025), Pratello 96/A-B "da Osvaldo" (2022–2024), pop-up contexts (Exposanità 2016, Guasto Village 2018), and an experimental workspace concept (JASPACE, 2024) — several of them legally distinct entities, all recognisably "L'Altro Spazio" in public identity.

This connects to **Proposition 3** ("A space, process or design communicates before a single word is spoken") — if the propositions are read as being about environments rather than a single building, the repeated pattern of building, losing, and rebuilding spaces becomes part of the evidence, not just a complication to be reconciled. This is offered as a hypothesis for the owner and for Working Paper v0.3 to test, not as an established institutional or legal fact, and it should not be used as public branding language before that review happens.
