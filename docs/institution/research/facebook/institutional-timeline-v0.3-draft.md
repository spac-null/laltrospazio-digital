## What changed from v0.2 — summary

1. **Four previously undocumented venues** now have a place in the institutional record: Via Polese 7 (2014), L'Altro Spazio al Pratello (2018-2025), L'Altro Spazio da Osvaldo (2022-2024), JASPACE (2024, unconfirmed).
2. **The 29 Oct 2015 Via Nazario opening date is now corroborated to near-DOCUMENTED strength** by the organisation's own contemporaneous public posts, not only by a decade-later interview memory.
3. **The ~2018 Farm→SRL Parco transition remains OWNER-CONFIRMED/approximate**, but the archive narrows the window (still Farm-branded as of Sept 2017) and separately shows that Farm's public cultural co-branding of Parco continued for years after any operational transfer — a nuance worth stating explicitly rather than treating brand persistence as contradicting the transition.
4. **One new, high-value cross-confirmation exists between the FB archive and a primary record**: the July 2024 "Coop L'Altro Spazio" Porto Verde credit line independently corroborates the May 2024 Cultura Verde award (S15/C009) with proof of delivery.
5. **The Comune di Bologna relationship around Parco has a longer, multi-episode history** than the working paper's single 2025-pact anchor point: an informal 2020 participatory-budget campaign and a 2023 "Diversity Team" collaboration both pre-date it.
6. **A ~3.5-month 2025 posting silence exactly spans the most significant documented institutional transition** — the clearest confirmation anywhere in the archive that public-communications silence must never be read as organisational inactivity for this account.
7. Two new specific, but **unverified**, leads were surfaced and should not be over-relied upon without corroboration: the 2017 "primo bar inclusivo d'Italia" claim, and the Oct 2022 "80% of staff" figure.

## What did not change

- The two-track practice/legal-entity model (Working Paper v0.2 §4) is confirmed, not revised, by everything in this archive.
- No claim in the main evidence register is CONTRADICTED by anything found here.
- The 2022-2026 "institutionalisation phase" framing (v0.2 §9, C046) is unaffected — if anything, the FB record shows the *practice* side of that framing (disability-policy convening, international art funding, formal employment/hiring language) maturing steadily well before 2022, which supports treating institutionalisation as building on, not creating, existing depth.
