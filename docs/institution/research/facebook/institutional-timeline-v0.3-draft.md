## What changed from v0.2 — summary

1. **Via Polese 7 (2014) is now a confirmed, owner-verified predecessor venue** — operated by Associazione L'Altro Spazio, the site of Jascha and Nunzia's first meeting, closed after water damage, and the direct cause of Via Nazario Sauro's founding as a new 50/50 SRL. No longer a research anomaly.
2. **Two further Pratello-area venues are now dated and entity-attributed**: L'Altro Spazio al Pratello (29/A, Dec 2018-Sept 2025) and "da Osvaldo" (96/A-B, 2022-2024), both operated through a single distinct entity, **L'Altro Spazio Pratello SRLS** (incorporated 25 May 2018, 50/50 Jascha Blume/Nunzia Vannuccini) — separate from L'Altro Spazio S.r.l. and not to be collapsed into it.
3. **JASPACE is downgraded** from an implied "fourth venue" to a January 2024 workspace concept/pilot whose implementation and duration are not established.
4. **The 29 Oct 2015 Via Nazario opening date is corroborated to near-DOCUMENTED strength** by the organisation's own contemporaneous public posts, and the deed-signing itself is now recorded as a vivid but clearly-labelled ATTRIBUTED/OWNER MEMORY, not converted into institutional fact.
5. **The ~2018 Farm→SRL Parco transition remains OWNER-CONFIRMED/approximate**; the archive narrows the window (still Farm-branded as of Sept 2017) and shows Farm's public cultural co-branding of Parco persisting for years after any operational transfer.
6. **The 2024 Cultura Verde picture is refined**: accounting/revenue inactivity and project/organisational activity are now explicitly distinguished, with the public contribution's actual financial liquidation dated to 2025.
7. **The 2025 ~3.5-month posting silence's cause is explicitly UNKNOWN** — the owner does not know why it occurred, and no cause (transition, crisis, closure, strategy) should be inferred. The silence remains useful only as a general methodological illustration.
8. **Two interpretive theses are proposed for Working Paper v0.3 review** (status: INTERPRETATION, not fact): that L'Altro Spazio may be better understood as a practice of environments than a single venue (connecting to Proposition 3); and that practice may sometimes run ahead of, or behind, the language used to describe it.
9. The 2017 "primo bar inclusivo d'Italia" claim and the Oct 2022 "80% of staff" figure remain **unverified self-reports** and should not be over-relied upon without independent corroboration.

## What did not change

- The two-track practice/legal-entity model (Working Paper v0.2 §4) is confirmed, not revised, by everything in this archive — if anything it now needs a *third* track (the Pratello SRLS) alongside the SRL and cooperative.
- No claim in the main evidence register is CONTRADICTED by anything found here.
- The 2022-2026 "institutionalisation phase" framing (v0.2 §9, C046) is unaffected — if anything, the FB record shows the *practice* side of that framing maturing steadily well before 2022.
- No confidential financial, legal-process, or personal detail from the confidential source material has been introduced into this file — only safe historical conclusions, labelled CONFIDENTIAL SUPPORT where applicable.

## Recommendation for Working Paper v0.3

Do not draft v0.3 yet, per the session brief. When it is drafted, it should:
- Add Via Polese as a substantive predecessor-venue and founding-origin section, not a footnote — including the water-damage closure and its direct causal link to the SRL/Via Nazario founding.
- Add the two Pratello-area venues and the Pratello SRLS entity to the institutional-lineage section (§4), clearly distinguished from L'Altro Spazio S.r.l. and the cooperative.
- Downgrade JASPACE to a proposed/experimental concept, not a venue.
- Add the July 2024 Porto Verde cross-confirmation to the evidence base (a new S-number in Appendix A, cross-referenced to C-FB-12), and explicitly separate 2024 accounting/revenue status from 2024 project/organisational activity.
- Consider adding an explicit methodological note that the organisation's public Facebook voice is structurally silent on governance/legal events, using the 2025 silence as the clearest illustration — while explicitly stating its cause is UNKNOWN and should not be speculated on further.
- Consider, carefully and only as a labelled INTERPRETATION, the "practice of environments" hypothesis (connecting to Proposition 3) and the "practice may run ahead of/behind language" hypothesis — both are research theses for owner and editorial review, not conclusions ready for public use.
- Preserve the oral-history value of the notary/bell founding memory without converting it into institutional fact.
- Continue excluding all financial, litigation, and personal-data detail behind the Pratello SRLS venues' history — the fact that "da Osvaldo" did not work is institutionally relevant; the private dossier behind it is not public content.
