# Programme / Practice Lifecycle Register

**Working date:** 14 August 2026
**Source:** Facebook archive 2014-2025 (930 posts, 200 hosted-event records, 7 albums). See `facebook-archive-inventory.md` for methodology and `facebook-archive-chronology.md` for the year-by-year narrative this register is drawn from.

For each programme: earliest archive evidence, later occurrences, frequency, apparent dormant periods, location(s), format changes, accessibility method (if stated/shown), associated people/organisations, last archive evidence, current/historical/unknown status, and evidence confidence. **First archive evidence is not treated as an actual start date** — where the practice is likely a continuation of something pre-dating the archive or a specific year's evidence, this is stated explicitly.

## 1. Cineporto / Cineporto BOff

- **Earliest evidence:** 7 Jun 2014 — a documentary by Jascha Blume ("I Sign, I Life") screened "al @cineporto_BOff." This is evidence of Cineporto's *existence* in 2014, not its origin — the account's own June 2016 retrospective describes Cineporto BOff at Parco 11 Settembre as already an established Associazione Farm project by the time of that post.
- **Later occurrences:** referenced as an established Farm project (Jun 2016); explicitly Farm/"Cine-Porto BOff"-branded as the identity of the Parco 11 Settembre summer season (Sept 2017, with L'Altro Spazio as thematic collaborator only); confirmed as a distinctly branded summer season with its own opening/closing rhythm (Jul 2019, "Ultima giornata al Parco 11 Settembre Cine-Porto BOff"); explicitly relaunched as "PARCO 11 EDIZIONE 2021… CinePorto Boff" (May-Jul 2021, Il Resto del Carlino coverage); `#CineportoBOFF` hashtag persists into 2023-24 posts alongside `#FARMAssociazioneCulturale` — five years after the owner-dated ~2018 Farm→SRL Parco operational transition.
- **Frequency:** annual summer season (roughly May-Jul/Aug), at least 2016, 2017, 2019, 2021, 2023-24 confirmed; 2018, 2020, 2022 seasons not directly evidenced in the read material but plausible given surrounding-year continuity (not confirmed — flag as a follow-up to check specifically).
- **Location:** Parco 11 Settembre.
- **Format:** cinema screenings, concerts, "incontri" (talks), aperitivi; a 2016 iteration ("Cinema dell'inclusione") was explicitly Deaf-subtitled and blind-audiodescribed.
- **Accessibility method:** subtitling for Deaf audiences + audiodescription for blind audiences (2016, explicit); otherwise the general Parco accessibility ethos (see §2).
- **People/organisations:** Associazione Farm (continuous, primary credited operator throughout, including 2023-24 hashtags); Jascha Blume (2014 filmmaker); L'Altro Spazio (thematic/disability-inclusion collaborator, 2017 onward).
- **Last archive evidence:** 2023-24 hashtag co-occurrence.
- **Status:** appears CURRENT (continuous branding through the archive's later years), though its precise 2025 status is not separately confirmed in the read material.
- **Evidence confidence:** FB-B (repeated across 6+ years, consistent branding); origin predates the archive.

## 2. Parco 11 Settembre

- **Earliest evidence:** implied as already active pre-2016 (Jun 2016 retrospective crediting Farm's "riqualificazione… del Parco 11 Settembre con il progetto Cine-Porto BOff" as prior work). Owner-confirmed history (per Working Paper v0.2, O01) dates the Farm-run strand to c. 2010 — the archive does not contradict this and offers no earlier direct evidence. (The archive's own 2014 opening is the separate Via Polese venue, which is a real predecessor L'Altro Spazio venue but a distinct thread from the Parco/Farm strand — see `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` C-FB-01.)
- **Later occurrences:** L'Altro Spazio as Farm's disability/inclusion-content collaborator (Sept 2017); continuous summer seasons at least 2019 (Cineporto BOff, Taiji Quan), 2020 (COVID-adapted, "Aperto tutti i giorni dalle 8.00 alle 00.00 fino al 20 settembre"), 2021 (CinePorto Boff edition, Redattore Sociale "acqua in LIS" quote), 2022 (day-trip events, EXOTICA relocates there for a summer edition), 2023 ("Rassegna in comune con Montagò," a new named summer co-production; season opens 29 Apr with "WE ARE OPEN"), 2024 ("Il Porto Ritrovato"/"Il Porto Verde di Bologna," see below).
- **Frequency:** an annual, seasonal (spring/summer) rhythm every year evidenced, without exception, 2016-2024.
- **Location:** Parco 11 Settembre 2001 (note: the account's own hashtags mis-transpose the year as "2011" at least twice in 2024 posts — an internal typo, not a substantive fact).
- **Format evolution:** from a Farm-branded cultural programme with L'Altro Spazio as collaborator (2016-17) → a shared/co-run seasonal venue with its own drinks/aperitivo service (2019 onward) → an explicitly cooperative-credited public-benefit project in 2024 ("Il Porto Verde di Bologna") → (per non-FB primary records) a formal 36-month Comune di Bologna collaboration pact from May 2025.
- **Accessibility/civic method:** 2020 — a first-person civic-engagement campaign (Nunzia Vannuccini's Nov 2020 post) supporting the park's candidacy in the Comune's 2020 **participatory budget** ("bilancio partecipativo") for "Manifattura 365" — direct evidence of a public-administration engagement channel around Parco **four to five years before** the working paper's earliest documented formal Comune/Parco agreement (May 2025). This is an important addition: the public-administration relationship around Parco did not begin in 2025; it has an earlier, informal civic-participation history visible only in the FB record.
- **People/organisations:** Associazione Farm (continuous, 2016-2024+); Comune di Bologna (Iperbole participatory platform, 2020; formal pact, 2025 per primary records); Montagò (2023 summer co-production); Ben Fatto - l'Altra scuola, L'Oca Verde, Associazione Officina Educativa (Jul 2024, "Il Porto Verde di Bologna").
- **Last archive evidence:** Jul 2024 ("Il Porto Ritrovato").
- **Status:** CURRENT and, per this archive, the single clearest through-line of institutional continuity across the entire 2014-2025 period, independent of which legal entity (Farm / SRL / cooperative) operated it in any given year.
- **Evidence confidence:** FB-A for the 2024 cooperative-credited project (dated, multi-partner, named); FB-B for annual continuity generally.

## 3. Cena al Buio (and its "al buio" format family)

- **Earliest evidence:** **10 & 17 Dec 2015**, as **"aperitivo al buio"** — *"Durante l'aperitivo il personale sarà non vedente e vi aiuterà a muovervi nell'ambiente"* (staff will be blind/low-vision and will guide you through the space) — sighted guests served and guided by non-sighted staff, not merely dining in darkness.
- **Later occurrences:** already a recurring, returning, TripAdvisor-corroborated format by **Feb 2016** ("Tornano le nostre cene al buio, due nel mese di febbraio") — with customer reviews quoted directly in posts (Apr 2016) — genuine third-party corroboration, not self-promotion. A related but distinct spin-off, **"cinema al buio"** (audiodescribed blind cinema, explicitly tied textually to Cineporto BOff — "waiting for BOff"), appears **9 May 2016**. Continues through 2017 with expanding dietary accommodation (vegan/vegetarian/celiac by May 2017). Rebrands mid-transition as **"Prove al Buio"** at the new Pratello venue (Dec 2018). **The exact name "Cena al Buio" consolidates by 20 Nov 2019.** Promised to return "da gennaio" at the newly-opened third venue (Oct 2022 post). **Not explicitly mentioned anywhere in the 2024 posts read** — a one-year apparent promotional gap. Returns with full branding and description for **Halloween 2025** (27-31 Oct): *"Spegni la luce, accendi i sensi"* ("Turn off the light, turn on the senses"), €25/person.
- **Frequency:** monthly-to-bimonthly through 2016-2019; presumed continuing 2022-23 (not directly quoted in the read 2023 material, flag as follow-up); apparent 2024 gap; confirmed 2025.
- **Location:** Via Nazario Sauro (origin, 2015-19); "L'Altro Spazio al Pratello" and "da Osvaldo" (promised 2023 continuation); Via Nazario Sauro again (2025).
- **Format:** dark/blindfolded dining or aperitivo, served/guided by staff who are themselves blind or low-vision — the format's defining and unchanged feature across its entire 10-year documented life is that the "service model," not just the "guest experience," inverts sightedness.
- **Related sibling formats:** "lettura al buio" (reading in the dark, with Centrodipoesia Bologna, recurring seasonally 2015-2017, described as having "l'ultimo della stagione" in Apr 2017 — i.e. a seasonal cadence, not a one-off); "cinema al buio" (2016, tied to Cineporto).
- **People/organisations:** in-house staff (explicitly described as blind/low-vision); Centrodipoesia Bologna (reading spin-off).
- **Last archive evidence:** 31 Oct 2025.
- **Status:** CURRENT, with one apparent (unexplained) 2024 gap in public promotion. One of the two or three most durable named formats in the entire archive — a decade of continuous or near-continuous operation with an unchanged core mechanism.
- **Evidence confidence:** FB-A/FB-B throughout (repeated, named, third-party TripAdvisor-corroborated in 2016; explicit halloween-2025 branding).

## 4. LIS / sign-language / Deaf-community activities

- **Earliest evidence:** indirect — the 2014 Via Polese venue's press coverage frames it around **deafblindness**, not LIS specifically; the earliest direct LIS-adjacent link is the 7 Jun 2014 Cineporto documentary about Deaf life. No explicit "LIS" course/class is evidenced at Via Nazario until later.
- **Later occurrences:** a recurring, seasonal **"corso di sensibilizzazione LIS"** (LIS-awareness course) run by **Ben Fatto - l'Altra scuola**, evidenced repeatedly across 2016-2017 (Sept 2016, Nov 2016, Jan 2017, Mar 2017) — open to "educatori e insegnanti" (educators/teachers), a genuine recurring training product, not a one-off. 2018-2019: comparatively **thinner** direct evidence — LIS/Deaf content in this window is more often third-party shared content (a sign-language classroom photo, a film share) than in-house programming; this is a fluctuation in visible emphasis, not a discontinuation (Deaf-focused content resumes strongly later). 25 Mar 2020: the account amplifies a public-service accessible-COVID-communications resource that includes **LIS video**. 15/17 Nov 2020: Nunzia Vannuccini's personal narrative states LIS is "la prima lingua di mia figlia" (her own daughter's first language) — the archive's most personally-grounded LIS testimony. 10 Jun 2021: an externally-sourced (Redattore Sociale) quote — children asking for water "in Lis" at the park kiosk — vivid evidence of ambient, spontaneous LIS use, not formal instruction. **3 Dec 2023: the richest formal LIS event in the archive** — *Il Piccolo Principe* presented in LIS by a University of Bologna LIS professor, plus a debate specifically on teaching LIS in primary schools, alongside the national **Ente Nazionale Sordi**. 2025: LIS reappears in internationally-funded, artistically sophisticated form — **"The Sub_Bar Show"** (Apr, touch/subwoofer-based art connecting Deaf and hearing audiences) and **"Sensory Dialogues"** (Apr, EU/Goethe-Institut-funded Deaf/hearing performance residency).
- **Frequency:** recurring seasonally as a course (2016-17); intermittent as event/policy content thereafter; the overall arc is toward **increasing formality and institutional sophistication** (a language-awareness course in 2016 → a university-and-national-institution-convened policy event in 2023 → internationally-funded art in 2025), even though raw posting frequency about LIS specifically does not increase monotonically.
- **Location:** primarily Via Nazario Sauro (courses, the 2023 event at Pratello 96/A); Parco 11 Settembre (the 2021 kiosk anecdote).
- **People/organisations:** Ben Fatto - l'Altra scuola (2016-17 course provider); Prof. Pietro Celo, Univ. of Bologna (2023); Ente Nazionale Sordi (2023); Sub_Bar/Eufonia, Culture Moves Europe/EU/Goethe-Institut (2025).
- **Last archive evidence:** Apr 2025 (Sensory Dialogues).
- **Status:** CURRENT, and the single clearest example in the archive of a practice that both **persists and matures** — the 2025 evidence is qualitatively more sophisticated than the 2016 evidence, not merely a repeat of it.
- **Evidence confidence:** FB-B (recurring course, 2016-17); FB-A (2023 event, 2025 EU-funded residency, both dated/detailed/named).

## 5. Exhibitions / visual art

- **Earliest evidence:** Nov 2015 — a wall-painting installation (Giulia Caliò, Anna Oppio, "The Honey Boat"/Alessandra Nardotto), completed 25 Nov 2015.
- **Later occurrences:** near-continuous through 2016-17 — "Negazione" (Jan 2016), "Le Stanze Illustrate" (Feb 2016), "Macrotema" (Apr-Jun 2016), Bilbolbul comic-festival tie-ins (Nov 2016, Jan-Feb 2017), a mural by Cyop&Kaf (Oct 2016), Jascha Blume's own installation "Gisteren Morgen" at SetUp Contemporary Art Fair (Jan 2017), "TRAME" (Jun 2017, ABABO fine-arts students), "Maheela" (May 2017, Nepal photography with **Braille captions via Istituto dei ciechi Francesco Cavazza** — an explicit accessible-exhibition accommodation). 2018-19: SetUp fair again (Feb 2018), Jascha Blume's "Hush – Dialoghi Possibili" at the new Pratello venue (Feb 2019). Continuous book-presentation programming throughout 2022-2025 (poetry, novels, disability-rights nonfiction).
- **Frequency:** effectively continuous/monthly across the whole archive.
- **Accessibility method:** Braille captions (2017); the venue's own physical-design accessibility (per Lonely Planet, 2018) as a constant backdrop to all exhibited work.
- **Status:** CURRENT throughout.
- **Evidence confidence:** FB-B (very high volume, consistent).

## 8. Employment / work-inclusion

- **Earliest evidence:** 1 May 2016 — explicit labour-rights framing (*"l'inclusione inizia proprio dal diritto al lavoro"*).
- **Later occurrences:** **17 Aug 2018**, in English — a full organisational hiring-policy statement distinguishing legal-quota compliance from stated practice ("we make our choice to hire people with a disability first, and not as a last resort"). **Oct 2022** — a specific, self-reported figure: *"clienti e lavoratrici e lavoratori con disabilità, che già oggi sono l'80% dello staff."*
- **Status:** the underlying employment practice is described as CURRENT at every point it is mentioned; no contradicting evidence found. The 80% figure is a strong claim that is entirely self-reported and not independently corroborated anywhere in this archive — treat as a lead for the evidence register, not a verified fact.
- **Evidence confidence:** FB-C for each individual statement (single posts); FB-D for the underlying practice's continuity (consistent framing across three widely-spaced years, 2016/2018/2022, with no contrary evidence).

## 11. Pop-ups / temporary and satellite venues

**Owner-correction update:** the Pratello-area venues are not a loose family of pop-ups sharing a name — they sit under a specific, distinct legal entity (**L'Altro Spazio Pratello SRLS**, incorporated 25 May 2018, owned 50/50 by Jascha Blume and Nunzia Vannuccini), separate from L'Altro Spazio S.r.l. The public "L'Altro Spazio" identity spans both entities; the entities themselves should not be collapsed into one another. See `facebook-historical-evidence-register.md` C-FB-07/C-FB-08 for status labelling.

- **Exposanità fair satellite bar** (May 2016, inside the BolognaFiere disability-expo hall) — FB-C, one confirmed year. (Operated under L'Altro Spazio S.r.l., predating the Pratello SRLS.)
- **Guasto Village container bar** (16 May-Oct 2018, university-district regeneration project) — FB-A, one confirmed season, framed as a distinct, temporary "Container bar," not a permanent venue.
- **L'Altro Spazio al Pratello** (Via del Pratello 29/A), operated through L'Altro Spazio Pratello SRLS — opened **December 2018** (OWNER-CONFIRMED, refining an initial owner recollection of ~December 2017, which is superseded rather than preserved as competing evidence — see the SRLS incorporation date above), formally inaugurated Jan-Feb 2019, operating continuously (confirmed at least 2019, 2020, 2022, 2023, and into Jan 2025). **OWNER-CONFIRMED: remained open until approximately the end of September 2025.** This is the one Pratello-area location that graduated from pop-up framing ("una scommessa," a bet/experiment) to a durable, near-seven-year second venue.
- **L'Altro Spazio da Osvaldo** (Via del Pratello 96/A-B) — a **further operating location of the same Pratello SRLS**, based on a business-lease arrangement (affitto di ramo d'azienda), not a separate naming partnership. OWNER-CONFIRMED working chronology: opened **2022**, discontinued **2024**, after proving economically unsustainable (costs materially higher than expected, income insufficient) — public-safe summary only; no financial, creditor, or legal-process detail is reproduced here (CONFIDENTIAL SUPPORT, see evidence register C-FB-08). *Source discrepancy preserved internally: one internal narrative places this location's opening in 2023 rather than 2022; the Facebook archive's own Oct 2022 opening post is consistent with 2022, which is used as the working chronology.*
- **JASPACE / coworking-workspace experiment** (Via del Pratello 96/C) — **prepared in January 2024** (an interest-form for potential users, with an intended near-term opening, alongside a related creative-workshop/"Studio Blume" concept). *Correction note: do not describe this as an established operating venue.* Sustained operation, duration, user base, and whether it moved beyond pilot stage are **not established**. Status: **PROPOSED / EXPERIMENTAL / IMPLEMENTATION UNCERTAIN**, not a confirmed fourth venue.
- None of Guasto Village, Pratello 29/A, or Da Osvaldo 96/A-B appear anywhere in Working Paper v0.2.

## Summary observation

Three programmes — **Cineporto/Cineporto BOff, Parco 11 Settembre, and Cena al Buio** — run continuously or near-continuously across the entire archive with no genuine dormant period longer than a single promotional gap (2024, for Cena al Buio). **Three confirmed venues** (Via Polese 7, 2014, now understood as a real predecessor venue operated by Associazione L'Altro Spazio; Pratello 29/A, 2018-2025; Pratello 96/A-B "da Osvaldo," 2022-2024, both under L'Altro Spazio Pratello SRLS) plus one unconfirmed pilot concept (JASPACE, 2024) are not documented anywhere in Working Paper v0.2 and deserve a place in any future institutional history. The LIS/Deaf-community thread is the clearest example of a practice that visibly *matures* in sophistication over the decade rather than simply repeating. A research hypothesis worth testing in Working Paper v0.3 (see `facebook-archive-chronology.md`, closing interpretive note): the venue turnover itself — building, losing, and rebuilding spaces under different legal vehicles while keeping one public identity — may be as institutionally characteristic of L'Altro Spazio as any single programme.
