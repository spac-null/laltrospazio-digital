Accessibility Information as Normal Design

The real, partially-unverified Via Nazario access profile (Current Public State v1 §3), shown as an ordinary page rather than a compliance dashboard. No wheelchair icon leading the design, no green-tick/red-cross coding, no separate "accessibility mode" — this is styled exactly like the rest of the site's fact-line and card language (Studies 1–2), because it must be.

Quick answers

Can I get in?

We can't yet confirm step-free access or door width for the current building. See what we know, and please ask us before you visit if this matters for you.

How can I communicate?

Text or WhatsApp works right now and is checked regularly. Whether LIS/sign-language support is available on your specific date hasn't been confirmed — ask us when you book.

What should I ask before coming?

Whether the entrance and toilet suit your needs, and whether a LIS-fluent host is on shift that day. Both are currently unconfirmed defaults, not confirmed "no"s.

What we know, dimension by dimension

Physical access— not yet confirmed

Entrance, door width, internal movement, counter height, seating and toilet accessibility haven't been checked for the current building. A 2015-era design intended a lowered counter, but that's a historical fact, not a current one — we won't guess.

Visual access— not yet confirmed

Lighting, contrast and menu format haven't been rechecked. Braille menus were documented in 2018; we don't yet know if that still holds today.

Deaf / communication access— text contact confirmed, staff coverage unconfirmed

Text and WhatsApp contact are confirmed and working. Whether a LIS-fluent host is on shift for your specific visit hasn't been confirmed — ask when you book.

Hearing / audio access— not established

Amplification, induction loop, and captioning haven't been established either way.

Cognitive / information access— not yet assessed

Clarity of directions and menu complexity haven't been assessed on-site yet.

Digital access— an engineering responsibility, not a claim

This website's own accessibility is something we're building to standard, not a claim we're making about the venue — it doesn't need a verification date the way a physical fact does.

Event-specific access— varies, check the listing

Some events note their own access conditions (interpretation, sensory intensity). Check the specific event you're interested in; nothing here should be assumed to apply to every event.

Assistance / contact— confirmed channel

Ask us anything on this page directly via WhatsApp; it's checked regularly. A separate, dedicated accessibility-question line hasn't been set up yet — this is the one to use for now.

Temporary limitations— none currently reported

Nothing is currently reported as out of service (no blocked route, no broken lift). If that changes, it will appear here as a dated notice, not silently.

Ask before you come

Most of what's above is honestly unconfirmed rather than confirmed absent. If any of it matters for your visit, message us and we'll check.
