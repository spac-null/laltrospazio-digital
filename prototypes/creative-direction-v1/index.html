Creative Direction v1 — Prototype Studies

Five focused, static-HTML studies testing whether Territory A (Measure) + Territory B (Encounter) actually produces a distinctive, hospitable, honest L'Altro Spazio digital environment — or collapses into something generic, cold, or a disguised copy of the book. See docs/design/laltrospazio-creative-prototype-test-v1.md for the full write-up and findings.

Not production. No framework, no build step, no dependency on src/. Palettes and typography systems are hypotheses under test, switchable above — nothing here is approved branding. Every study uses real, public-safe, already-established L'Altro Spazio facts; where a fact is unknown, it is shown as unknown.

Study 1 — Current / Archive Marker

Three treatments distinguishing "happening now" from "history," tested against the same four real facts.

Study 2 — Freshness / Uncertainty Display

Translating the content model's evidence discipline into plain, non-bureaucratic sentences.

Study 3 — Two-Voice Typography

Three typographic systems for the functional/editorial tension, tested across real content kinds.

Study 4 — Accessibility Information as Normal Design

The real, partially-unverified nine-dimension access profile, styled like an ordinary page.

Study 5 — Relationship / Multiple Routes

Linear vs. spatial treatment of one Cena al Buio content cluster — testing whether relational layout earns its keep.
