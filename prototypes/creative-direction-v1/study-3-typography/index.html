Long-form paragraph — editorial voice at reading measure

L'Altro Spazio is a bar, cultural venue and social cooperative in Bologna where disabled and Deaf people work as hosts, cooks, artists and designers — not as guests being accommodated. It grew out of two people's different routes into the same realisation: that exclusion usually isn't caused by disability itself, but by spaces, services and assumptions built without disabled people in mind. Today that practice runs through a venue on Via Nazario Sauro, seasonal work at Parco 11 Settembre, and a cooperative that is turning a decade of informal practice into a more durable institution.
