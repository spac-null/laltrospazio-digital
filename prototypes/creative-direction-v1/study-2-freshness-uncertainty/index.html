Study 2 of 5

Freshness / Uncertainty Display

The content model tracks status (current / historical / submitted…) and freshness (verified / needs-verification / stale) as internal evidence metadata. This study tests whether that discipline can reach a visitor as plain, calm English/Italian sentences — never as visible research jargon. Each block below has a "for reviewers" disclosure showing the internal label it maps from; that disclosure is a QA aid for this prototype only and would not exist in production.
