- Inspect before asking. Use the repo, config, docs, and live public evidence first.
- Project documentation in this repo is canonical; conversation history is not.
- Read `docs/project/current-state.md` before significant work.
- Update `docs/project/current-state.md` after meaningful work.
- Never invent public facts. Distinguish verified, owner-confirmed, externally-supported, unverified, conflicting, outdated, and remove.
- Preserve accessibility as a design-system requirement, not a marketing layer.
- Do not expose secrets in code, docs, logs, screenshots, or generated output.
- Do not push or deploy exploratory work to production.
- Do not modify external business accounts or profiles without explicit authorization.
- Do not spend advertising money without explicit authorization.
- Minimize recurring human procedures. Prefer automation for deterministic recurring work.
- Preserve and record pre-existing dirty working-tree changes.
- Never stage or commit unrelated pre-existing changes.
- Verify the staged diff before committing.
- Build and test relevant changes before considering work complete.
